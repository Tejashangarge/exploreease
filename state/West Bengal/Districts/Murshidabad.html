Welcome to Murshidabad

The Royal Seat of Bengal’s Nawabs

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Central West Bengal, India
  • On the banks of: Bhagirathi River
  • Language: Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, English

🏰 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former capital of Bengal, Bihar, and Odisha
  • Seat of the Nawabs before British rule
  • Crucial site of the Battle of Plassey (1757)

🌿 Heritage & Scenic Attractions

  • Hazarduari Palace – iconic thousand-door palace
  • Katra Mosque and Nawabi-era monuments
  • River ghats and historical cemeteries

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Rail and road connectivity from Kolkata
  • Heritage hotels, guest houses, and lodges

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Hazarduari Palace Magnificent palace with 1,000 doors and museum
Katra Mosque Historic mosque built by Murshid Quli Khan
Nizamat Imambara One of the largest imambaras in India
Motijheel Historic pleasure garden and Nawabi residence
