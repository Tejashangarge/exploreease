Welcome to Howrah

Gateway to Kolkata & Home of the Iconic Howrah Bridge

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Western bank of the Hooghly River, West Bengal
  • District: Howrah
  • Language: Bengali, Hindi, English

🏗️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Howrah Bridge – one of the busiest cantilever bridges in the world
  • Major railway junction connecting Eastern India
  • Rich industrial and engineering heritage

🌿 Natural & Urban Attractions

  • Hooghly River ghats and ferry rides
  • Botanical Garden – home to the Great Banyan Tree
  • Riverside cityscapes and heritage structures

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Well-connected by rail, road, metro, and ferries
  • Budget hotels and lodges near transport hubs

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Howrah Bridge Iconic steel bridge and lifeline of the city
Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Botanical Garden Historic garden with the famous Great Banyan Tree
Belur Math Spiritual center founded by Swami Vivekananda
Howrah Station One of the oldest and busiest railway stations in India
