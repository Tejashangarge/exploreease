Welcome to Siliguri

The Gateway to North-East India & the Eastern Himalayas

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Northern West Bengal, India
  • Region: Foothills of the Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Bengali, Nepali, Hindi, English

🌏 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Strategic trade and transport hub of North Bengal
  • Melting pot of Bengali, Nepali, Tibetan, and North-Eastern cultures
  • Important junction for Sikkim, Bhutan, and the Northeast

🌿 Natural & Urban Attractions

  • Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Teesta & Mahananda river views
  • Salugara Monastery and city viewpoints

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Bagdogra Airport – major regional air hub
  • Well-connected by rail and national highways
  • Wide range of hotels, lodges, and business stays

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary Dense forest reserve rich in wildlife and bird species
Salugara Monastery Peaceful Buddhist monastery near the city
Hong Kong Market Popular shopping hub for tourists
North Bengal Science Centre Educational and interactive science exhibits
