Welcome to Kolkata

The City of Joy — Heritage, Culture & Modern Life

📍 Overview

  • Location: Eastern India, West Bengal
  • Population: 14+ million metropolitan
  • Language: Bengali, English, Hindi

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Capital of British India until 1911
  • Birthplace of Bengali Renaissance
  • Home to historic colonial architecture

🌆 Attractions & Landmarks

  • Howrah Bridge – iconic engineering marvel
  • Victoria Memorial – grand colonial monument
  • Indian Museum – India’s oldest museum
  • Marble Palace & Kalighat Temple – heritage and devotion

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport
  • Extensive metro, rail, and bus connectivity
  • Hotels ranging from budget to luxury 5-star

⭐ Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Victoria Memorial Grand marble monument with museum and gardens
Howrah Bridge World-famous cantilever bridge over Hooghly River
Indian Museum Historic museum with vast cultural & archaeological collections
Kalighat Temple Ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Kali
