Land of Devotion, Learning & the Bhakti Movement

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Central West Bengal, India
  • Headquarters: Krishnanagar
  • Language: Bengali, Hindi, English

📜 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu
  • Center of the Vaishnava Bhakti movement
  • Historic seat of learning – Nadia School of Logic (Navya Nyaya)

🌿 Spiritual & Natural Attractions

  • Nabadwip – sacred pilgrimage town on the Ganga
  • ISKCON Mayapur – global spiritual center
  • Krishnanagar Rajbari and Ghurni clay art village

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Well connected by rail and road from Kolkata
  • Hotels, dharamshalas, and guest houses for pilgrims

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mayapur (ISKCON) International spiritual hub and temple complex
Nabadwip Birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu
Krishnanagar Rajbari Royal palace reflecting Bengal architecture
Ghurni Famous for traditional clay idol craftsmanship
