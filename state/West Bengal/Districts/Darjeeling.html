Welcome to Darjeeling

The Queen of the Himalayas

🏔️ Overview

  • Location: Northern West Bengal, India
  • Elevation: ~2,045 meters above sea level
  • Language: Nepali, Bengali, Hindi, English

🌸 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Famous for world-renowned Darjeeling Tea
  • Colonial-era hill station established by the British
  • Blend of Nepali, Tibetan, and Bengali cultures

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Tiger Hill – sunrise views of Kanchenjunga
  • Tea gardens with panoramic mountain scenery
  • Batasia Loop – scenic railway marvel

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, homestays, and eco-resorts
  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (UNESCO World Heritage)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Tiger Hill Iconic sunrise point with Himalayan views
Darjeeling Tea Gardens Rolling green estates producing famous tea
Batasia Loop Spiral railway track with mountain backdrop
Peace Pagoda Symbol of harmony with serene surroundings
