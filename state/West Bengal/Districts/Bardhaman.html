Welcome to Bardhaman

A Historic City with Cultural Roots & Modern Growth

📍 Overview

  • Location: Central West Bengal, India
  • District: Purba Bardhaman
  • Languages: Bengali, Hindi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former kingdom of the Bardhaman Maharajas
  • Important administrative center during British rule
  • Blend of heritage, education, and agriculture

🌆 Attractions & Landmarks

  • Curzon Gate – iconic colonial structure
  • Bardhaman Rajbari and museum
  • 108 Shiv Mandir temple complex

🚆 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Major railway junction connecting Kolkata & North Bengal
  • Good road connectivity and urban facilities

⭐ Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Curzon Gate Symbolic entrance to the city with colonial design
Bardhaman Rajbari Historic palace of the Bardhaman royal family
108 Shiv Mandir Unique circular temple architecture
Damodar River Important river shaping the region’s geography
