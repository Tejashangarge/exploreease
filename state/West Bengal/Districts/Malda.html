Welcome to Malda

Land of Ancient Capitals & Famous Mangoes

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Northern West Bengal, India
  • Region: Historical Gour–Pandua area
  • Language: Bengali, Hindi, English

🏰 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Once the capital of ancient Bengal Sultanate
  • Historic ruins of Gour and Pandua
  • Renowned for silk and mango cultivation

🌿 Natural & Cultural Attractions

  • Gour ruins – mosques, gateways, and madrasas
  • Pandua archaeological site
  • Mango orchards and river landscapes

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Rail and road connectivity to major Bengal cities
  • Budget hotels and lodges for travelers

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Adina Mosque Largest mosque built in medieval India
Dakhil Darwaza Grand gateway of ancient Gour city
Pandua Ruins Historic Islamic and Hindu monuments
Malda Mango Orchards Famous for varieties like Fazli and Himsagar
