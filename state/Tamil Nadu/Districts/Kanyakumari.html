Welcome to Kanyakumari

India's Southernmost Tip - Where Three Seas Meet

🌅 Overview

  • Location: Southern tip of Indian peninsula, Tamil Nadu
  • Region: Confluence of Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal & Indian Ocean
  • Language: Tamil, English, Malayalam

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Kanyakumari Bhagavathy Temple - 3000-year-old Devi shrine
  • Vivekananda Rock Memorial - Swami Vivekananda's meditation site
  • Thiruvalluvar Statue - 133ft Tamil poet monument

🌺 Natural Attractions

  • Spectacular sunrise & sunset at three seas confluence
  • Sanguthurai & Sothavilai beaches nearby
  • Mathoor Hanging Trough - Asia's longest aqueduct

⛴️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Ferry services to Vivekananda Rock & Thiruvalluvar Statue
  • Gandhi Memorial & Kamarajar Memorial Mandapams
  • Nearby Padmanabhapuram Palace & Suchindram Temple

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Vivekananda Rock Memorial Swami Vivekananda attained enlightenment
Thiruvalluvar Statue 133ft statue of Tamil poet-philosopher
Kanyakumari Temple Ancient Devi Kanyakumari Amman shrine
