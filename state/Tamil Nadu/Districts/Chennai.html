Welcome to Chennai

Gateway to South India - Detroit of India

🌊 Overview

  • Location: Coromandel Coast, Tamil Nadu
  • Region: Capital of Tamil Nadu state
  • Language: Tamil, English, Telugu, Hindi

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Fort St. George - 1644 British settlement origin
  • Ancient temples of Pallavas, Cholas, Pandyas
  • Birthplace of Bharatanatyam & Carnatic music

🏙️ Natural Attractions

  • Marina Beach - World's 2nd longest urban beach
  • Guindy National Park in city center
  • Elliott's Beach & Besant Nagar promenade

🚀 Tourism Infrastructure

  • International airport & major seaport
  • IT hub with global companies
  • Auto manufacturing capital of India
