Welcome to Madurai

Athens of the East - Cultural Capital of Tamil Nadu

🌟 Overview

  • Location: South-central Tamil Nadu, Vaigai River
  • Region: Ancient Pandyan kingdom capital
  • Language: Tamil, English, Telugu

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Meenakshi Temple - 14 magnificent gopurams
  • Thirumalai Nayak Palace - Indo-Saracenic marvel
  • Sangam literature & Tamil learning center

🌿 Natural Attractions

  • Mariamman Teppakulam tank & Chithirai festival
  • Nearby hill stations Kodaikanal & Theni
  • Vandiyur Mariamman temple complex

✈️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • International airport & major railway junction
  • Heritage hotels & temple stay facilities
  • Jallikattu events & Chithirai car festival

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Meenakshi Temple 17th century, 14 gopurams, 33,000 sq ft
Thirumalai Nayak Palace 1636 Nayak marvel with 1000 pillars
Alagar Kovil Hilltop Vishnu temple with waterfalls
Gandhi Memorial Museum India's first memorial museum (1840 building)
