Chennai – Gateway of the South

Chennai, formerly Madras, is Tamil Nadu's capital and a major cultural, economic and educational hub on the Coromandel Coast, known for its beaches, temples, classical arts and colonial heritage.

Beaches • Temples • Culture • Marina

Major attractions & experiences

Visitors enjoy Chennai's iconic Marina Beach, ancient Kapaleeshwarar Temple, colonial Fort St. George, and cultural venues showcasing Carnatic music, Bharatanatyam dance and Tamil cinema heritage.

The city offers government museum, snake park, island beaches like Elliot's and Covelong, plus nearby temple towns like Mahabalipuram and Kanchipuram.

Marina Beach
World's second longest beach, iconic promenade with statues, food stalls and evening crowds.
Kapaleeshwarar Temple
Stunning Dravidian architecture Shiva temple in Mylapore with towering gopuram and sacred tank.
Fort St. George & Museum
17th-century British fort housing the first British settlement and state museum with rare artifacts.
Government Museum & Arts
Asia's oldest museum complex with bronze gallery, art collections and cultural performances.

City overview & history

Chennai is one of India's largest cities and the fourth most populous metropolis, serving as the capital of Tamil Nadu and a key port city on the Bay of Bengal.

Founded in the 17th century by the British East India Company, the city grew from a cluster of fishing villages into a major colonial center, later becoming a hub of Tamil culture, cinema and industry.

Known as the "Detroit of India" for its automobile industry and "India's health capital" due to world-class medical tourism facilities.

Quick facts

  • Region: Coromandel Coast, eastern Tamil Nadu.
  • Key identity: Cultural capital, IT hub and major port city.
  • Population: Over 12 million in metro area.
  • Languages: Tamil, English, Telugu and others.

Climate & best time to visit

Chennai has a tropical climate with hot and humid weather throughout the year, moderated by sea breezes, with a distinct northeast monsoon season.

The best time for comfortable sightseeing is from November to February when temperatures are pleasant and humidity is lower.

Summer months (April-June) are very hot with temperatures often exceeding 38°C, while October-December monsoon brings heavy rains to the city.

Best season: Nov – Feb.
Summer highs: 38–42°C.
Monsoon: Oct – Dec.

Culture, food & lifestyle

Chennai is the heart of Tamil culture, birthplace of Carnatic music, Bharatanatyam dance and Tamil cinema industry, with vibrant festivals like Pongal, Marina music season and temple celebrations.

Famous for South Indian cuisine including idli, dosa, filter coffee and Chettinad specialties, with bustling food streets like Triplicane and T. Nagar.

Traditional silk sarees, Kanchipuram weaves, Tanjore paintings and bronze sculptures represent the city's rich artisanal heritage.

Why Chennai stands out

  • India's cultural capital with living classical arts traditions.
  • Gateway city connecting to Tamil Nadu's temple circuit and beaches.
  • Unique blend of colonial history, Dravidian architecture and modern IT hub.

Travel tips for visitors

Plan 3–4 days exploring central Chennai's temples and museums, Marina Beach evenings, plus day trips to Mahabalipuram or Pondicherry using metro, buses or cabs.

  • Winter months ideal for temple visits, beach walks and cultural events; carry light cotton clothes.
  • Visit temples early morning to avoid crowds and heat; modest clothing required.
  • Monsoon brings heavy rain affecting beach visits; check tide conditions for coastal areas.
