Welcome to Durg City

Steel city hub and cultural heart of Chhattisgarh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Part of Durg-Bhilai urban agglomeration
  • Language: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to India's largest steel plant (Bhilai)
  • Ancient temples and JAIN pilgrimage sites
  • Rich tribal and industrial heritage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Tandula Dam - scenic reservoir and picnic spot
  • Shivnath River - beautiful riverfront views
  • Maitri Bagh - zoo and landscaped gardens

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels near Bhilai Steel Plant area
  • Railway junction and road connectivity

Attraction Description
