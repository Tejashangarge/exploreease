Shakti Peeth and tribal heritage of Bastar region

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southern Bastar, Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Part of Bastar tribal belt
  • Language: Hindi, Halbi, Gondi

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Danteshwari Temple - one of 52 Shakti Peethas
  • Ancient Barsoor ruins - 200+ temples
  • Rich Gond tribal culture and traditions

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Shankini-Dankini river confluence
  • Bailadila hills iron ore mines
    • Chitrakote Waterfalls nearby

    🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

    • Buses from Raipur, Jagdalpur
    • Dantewada Railway Station
    • Hotels and dharamshalas near temple

    📍 Must-Visit Attractions

    Attraction Description
    Danteshwari Temple Major Shakti Peetha temple complex
    Barsoor Temples Ancient ruins with 200+ temples
    Shankini-Dankini Ghat Sacred river confluence point
    Bhadrakali Temple Ancient temple in scenic location
