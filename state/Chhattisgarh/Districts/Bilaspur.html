Welcome to Bilaspur City

Explore the judicial capital and natural wonders of Chhattisgarh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Judicial capital of Chhattisgarh
  • Language: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ratanpur - ancient capital of Kalchuri dynasty
  • Malhar - archaeological treasures and ancient temples
  • Mahamaya Temple - major pilgrimage center

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Achanakmar Tiger Reserve - UNESCO biosphere reserve
  • Bilasa Tal - scenic lake with boating
  • Kanan Pendari Zoo Park - wildlife and nature trails

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts and budget accommodations
  • Bilaspur Airport and railway connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Achanakmar Wildlife Sanctuary Tiger reserve with dense forests and wildlife
Mahamaya Temple, Ratanpur Ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Mahamaya
Madku Dweep Island temple complex in Shivnath River
Bilasa Tal Beautiful lake with boating facilities
