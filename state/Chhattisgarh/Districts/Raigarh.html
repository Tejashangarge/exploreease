Welcome to Raigarh City

Cultural capital and silk weaving hub of Chhattisgarh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Cultural Capital of Chhattisgarh
  • Language: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Famous for Dhokra metal casting and silk weaving
  • Former princely state with royal heritage
  • Rajim Kumbh Mela - major religious festival

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Kelo River - scenic riverfront and ghats
  • Bar lake and surrounding green landscapes
  • Forested plateaus and hill ranges

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts and heritage homestays
  • Railway junction and road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Rajim Temple Complex Site of famous Kumbh Mela festival
Baradari Maidan Historic park with royal pavilion
Kelo River Ghat Beautiful riverfront temple area
Silk Weaving Centre Traditional Tasar silk production
