Welcome to Bastar

Tribal heartland and nature's paradise of Chhattisgarh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southern Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Cultural capital & tribal heartland
  • Language: Hindi, Halbi, Gondi, Bhatri

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • World famous Bastar Dussehra festival
  • Rich tribal culture of Gond, Maria tribes
  • Danteshwari Temple - kuldevi of Bastar

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Chitrakote Waterfall - Niagara of India
  • Kanger Valley National Park - biodiversity hotspot
  • Kutumsar & Kailash Caves - mysterious underground

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Tribal resorts, jungle lodges & homestays
  • Jagdalpur Airport connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Chitrakote Waterfall Horseshoe shaped - India's widest waterfall
Tirathgarh Waterfall Majestic falls with ancient Shiva temple
Kanger Valley NP Ancient caves, wildlife & pristine forests
Danteshwari Temple Sacred temple in Jagdalpur city center
