Welcome to Korba City

Power capital and industrial hub of Chhattisgarh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North-central Chhattisgarh, India
  • Region: Power capital of Chhattisgarh
  • Language: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Tribal culture of Korwa (Pahadi Korwa) people
  • Hasdeo River confluence with Ahiran River
  • Rich forest heritage and tribal traditions

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Hasdeo River - scenic riverfront landscapes
  • Lush green forests covering 40% of district
  • Pond wildlife sanctuaries nearby

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels near power plants and industrial areas
  • Rail connectivity and good road networks

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Hasdeo Riverfront Beautiful confluence point with scenic views
Sirpur (nearby) Ancient Buddhist site with archaeological ruins
Pond Wildlife Sanctuary Rich biodiversity and nature trails
