Major attractions & experiences

Hyderabad combines historic monuments, palaces and tombs with lakes, parks, film studios and modern entertainment zones.

Popular experiences span Charminar and Laad Bazaar, Golconda Fort and Qutb Shahi Tombs, Hussain Sagar Lake and Ramoji Film City, along with museums and food streets.

City overview & history

Hyderabad stands on the banks of the Musi River and was founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah of the Qutb Shahi dynasty, with Charminar as its central landmark.

Under the Asaf Jahi Nizams it grew into a major centre for pearls, textiles and Indo‑Islamic culture, giving it the names "City of Pearls" and "City of Nizams".

Today Hyderabad combines heritage quarters in the old city with modern IT corridors like HITEC City and Gachibowli.

Quick facts

  • Capital and largest city of Telangana.
  • Nicknames: City of Pearls, City of Nizams.
  • Key sectors: IT, pharma, biotech, film, trade and tourism.
  • Languages: Telugu and Urdu official; English widely used.

Climate & best time to visit

Hyderabad has hot summers, a humid monsoon driven by southwest winds and generally mild, dry winters.

Travel guides usually recommend visiting between November and February, when temperatures are more comfortable for walking tours and outdoor food or lake visits.

Summer can see daytime readings above 40 degrees Celsius, while monsoon showers from June to September moderate heat but increase humidity.

Best season: Nov – Feb.
Summer highs: Around 40–45°C.
Winter range: Roughly 13–29°C.

Culture, food & economy

Hyderabad's culture blends Deccani, Telugu and Urdu influences, visible in its architecture, language, literature and festivals.

The city's food identity is defined by Hyderabadi biryani, haleem during Ramadan, kebabs and rich desserts, supported by a strong café and street‑food culture.

As a major IT and pharmaceutical hub with global firms and tech parks, Hyderabad also attracts students, professionals and entrepreneurs from all over India and abroad.

What makes Hyderabad special?

  • Historic royal city that evolved into a leading IT and innovation centre.
  • Internationally famous for biryani and diverse street food culture.
  • Compact mix of monuments, museums, lakes, malls and entertainment parks.

Travel tips for visitors

Travellers often plan 3–4 days for Charminar, old city walks, Golconda Fort, Hussain Sagar, museums and a full‑day visit to Ramoji Film City or nearby parks.

  • Choose winter months for walking tours and fort climbs with comfortable temperatures.
  • Use metro, buses, cabs and autos; narrow old‑city lanes are easier on foot or in auto‑rickshaws.
  • Visit Charminar and food lanes in the evening for lights and snacks, and time Golconda for late‑afternoon or sunset views.
