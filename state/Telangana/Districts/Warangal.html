Welcome to Warangal

Uncover the historical grandeur and natural beauty of Telangana

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northeastern Telangana, India
  • Region: Warangal District
  • Language: Telugu, Urdu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Capital of the Kakatiya dynasty with rich medieval heritage
  • Famous for intricate stone architecture and temple carvings
  • UNESCO tentative site for its cultural monuments

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Pakhal Lake – serene reservoir surrounded by forested hills
  • Eturnagaram Wildlife Sanctuary – home to diverse flora and fauna

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts, and heritage stays available across the city
  • Connected via Warangal Railway Station and national highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Warangal Fort Historic fort with massive stone gateways and Kakatiya architecture
Thousand Pillar Temple Ancient temple dedicated to Shiva, Vishnu, and Surya with exquisite carvings
Bhadrakali Temple Spiritual site overlooking a lake, dedicated to Goddess Bhadrakali
Pakhal Lake Scenic lake ideal for picnics and nature walks
