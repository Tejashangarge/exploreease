Welcome to Adilabad

Discover the tribal heritage and natural wonders of northern Telangana

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Telangana, India
  • Region: Adilabad District
  • Language: Telugu, Marathi, Urdu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to diverse tribal communities and rich folklore traditions
  • Influences from Marathi and Telugu cultures
  • Known for Dokra metal craft and traditional art forms

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Kuntala Waterfalls – tallest waterfall in Telangana, nestled in forested hills
  • Pochera Falls – wide and scenic cascade surrounded by greenery
  • Kawal Tiger Reserve – sanctuary for wildlife and eco-tourism

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Basic hotels, forest lodges, and eco-tourism facilities available
  • Connected via Adilabad Railway Station and national highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kuntala Waterfalls Majestic waterfall ideal for nature lovers and photographers
Pochera Falls Broad waterfall with scenic views and picnic spots
Kawal Tiger Reserve Protected forest area with tigers, leopards, and rich biodiversity
Basar Saraswathi Temple Famous temple dedicated to Goddess Saraswathi, near Adilabad
