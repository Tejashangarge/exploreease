Welcome to Bhadradri Kothagudem

Journey through the spiritual, tribal, and natural heart of Telangana

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Telangana, India
  • Region: Bhadradri Kothagudem District
  • Language: Telugu, Koya, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the sacred Bhadrachalam Temple dedicated to Lord Rama
  • Rich tribal heritage, especially of the Koya community
  • Historical significance in the coal mining and forest conservation movements

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Papi Hills – scenic river gorge along the Godavari River
  • Kinnerasani Dam & Wildlife Sanctuary – lush forests and wildlife

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Temple guest houses, forest lodges, and budget hotels available
  • Connected via Bhadrachalam Road Railway Station and state highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Bhadrachalam Temple Famous temple of Lord Rama, a major pilgrimage site on the banks of the Godavari
Papi Hills Stunning hill range with boat rides and river views through lush valleys
Kinnerasani Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area with a dam, deer park, and scenic viewpoints
Coal Mines of Kothagudem Historic coal mining region with industrial and economic significance
