Welcome to Khammam

Explore the historical forts, lush landscapes, and cultural richness of Telangana

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Telangana, India
  • Region: Khammam District
  • Language: Telugu, Urdu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Khammam Fort – a 1000-year-old fort built by the Kakatiya dynasty
  • Blend of tribal heritage and Telugu culture
  • Important center during the Telangana movement

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Palair Lake – a scenic reservoir ideal for boating and picnics
  • Kinnerasani Wildlife Sanctuary – home to deer, leopards, and migratory birds

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guest houses, and eco-tourism resorts available
  • Well-connected via Khammam Railway Station and national highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Khammam Fort Historic hilltop fort with panoramic views and ancient architecture
Palair Lake Popular spot for boating, birdwatching, and relaxing by the water
Kinnerasani Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area with rich biodiversity and a tranquil reservoir
Nelakondapalli Ancient Buddhist site with stupas and archaeological significance
