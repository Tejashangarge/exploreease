Welcome to Muzaffarpur City

Explore the Litchi Kingdom of Bihar - Nature & Heritage

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: North Bihar, India
  • Region: Tirhut division
  • Language: Hindi, Bhojpuri, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • World famous for Shahi Litchi orchards
  • Birthplace of freedom fighter Khudiram Bose
  • Rich cultural heritage and temples

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Motijheel Lake – scenic boating and walks
  • Litchi gardens during fruit season

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and resorts near litchi orchards
  • Excellent rail connectivity (Muzaffarpur Junction)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Garib Sthan Mandir Famous temple complex with spiritual significance
Motijheel Picturesque lake perfect for boating
Jubba Sahni Park Popular recreational park with gardens
Khudiram Bose Memorial Tribute to young freedom fighter
