Welcome to Arrah City

Land of Veer Kunwar Singh - Freedom & Heritage

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Western Bihar, India
  • Region: Bhojpur district
  • Language: Bhojpuri, Hindi, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birthplace of freedom fighter Veer Kunwar Singh
  • Key center during 1857 Revolt against British
  • Rich Bhojpuri culture and heritage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ganga & Son rivers confluence – scenic riverfront
  • Fertile agricultural plains and green fields

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and lodges near railway station
  • Excellent rail connectivity (Arrah Junction)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Jagdishpur Fort Home of Veer Kunwar Singh - 1857 revolt site
Aranya Devi Temple Ancient temple with spiritual significance
Bhojeshwar Temple Historic temple architecture
Krishna Museum Local history and artifacts museum
