Welcome to Gaya City

Explore the spiritual heart of Bihar - Land of Enlightenment

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Central Bihar, India
  • Region: Magadh
  • Language: Hindi, Magahi, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birthplace of Buddhism - Where Buddha attained enlightenment
  • One of Hinduism's most sacred pilgrimage sites
  • Ancient city with deep spiritual and historical roots

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Falgu River – sacred river for pilgrimage rituals
  • Pretshila Hill – scenic views and temple complex

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, dharamshalas and guesthouses for pilgrims
  • Rail, air connectivity via Gaya Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mahabodhi Temple UNESCO site where Buddha attained enlightenment
Vishnupad Temple Sacred temple with Lord Vishnu's footprint
Mangla Gauri Temple One of 51 Shakti Peethas, ancient temple
Barabar Caves Ancient rock-cut caves from Mauryan era
