Welcome to Darbhanga City

Cultural Capital of Bihar - Heart of Mithila

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: North Bihar, India
  • Region: Mithilanchal
  • Language: Maithili, Hindi, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Cultural capital and former seat of Darbhanga Raj
  • Birthplace of Maithili language and literature
  • Famous for Mithila painting and Madhubani art

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Bagmati River – scenic riverfront and ghats
  • Lush green fields and rural landscapes

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and heritage homestays
  • Rail connectivity (Darbhanga Junction)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Rambagh Palace Grand palace of Darbhanga Maharajas
Kameshwari Temple Ancient temple complex
Shyama Mai Temple Popular temple with spiritual significance
Anand Bagh Palace Historic royal residence
