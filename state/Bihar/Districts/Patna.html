Welcome to Patna City

Explore the historical and spiritual heart of Bihar

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Bihar, India
  • Region: Magadh
  • Language: Hindi, Bhojpuri, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ancient Pataliputra - Capital of Mauryan Empire
  • Birthplace of Buddhism and Jainism
  • Land of Emperor Ashoka and great historical sites

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ganges River – sacred ghats and riverfront walks
  • Golghar granary with panoramic views

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and guesthouses for all budgets
  • Rail, air, and road connectivity
