Welcome to Nagpur

The Orange City of India – vibrant, green, and full of life

📍 Overview

  • Location: Central India, Maharashtra
  • Region: Vidarbha
  • Language: Marathi, Hindi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Highlights

  • Deekshabhoomi – sacred Buddhist monument
  • Zero Mile Stone – geographical center of India
  • Rich heritage in cotton trade and tiger conservation

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Ambazari Lake and Garden – popular picnic spot
  • Nearby tiger reserves – Tadoba, Pench, Bor

🚆 Travel & Infrastructure

  • Major railway junction and airport
  • Metro rail and excellent road connectivity
