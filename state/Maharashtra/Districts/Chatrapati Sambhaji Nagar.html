📍 Overview

  • Location: Central Maharashtra
  • Region: Marathwada
  • Language: Marathi, Hindi, Urdu, English

🏰 Historical & Cultural Significance

  • Ajanta & Ellora Caves – UNESCO World Heritage Sites
  • Bibi Ka Maqbara – Mughal-era monument resembling the Taj Mahal
  • Daulatabad Fort – 14th-century fort city

🌿 Natural Beauty

  • Godavari River basin and monsoon-driven climate
  • Scenic hills and rural landscapes

🚆 Travel & Infrastructure

  • Rail and road connectivity to major cities
  • Nearby airport with flights to Mumbai, Delhi, and Hyderabad

🎯 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ajanta Caves Rock-cut Buddhist monuments from the 2nd century BCE
Ellora Caves Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain temples carved into cliffs
Bibi Ka Maqbara Elegant Mughal tomb built by Aurangzeb
Daulatabad Fort Strategic hill fort with panoramic views
