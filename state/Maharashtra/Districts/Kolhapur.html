Welcome to Kolhapur

Where tradition, temples, and taste come together

📍 Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Maharashtra
  • Region: Sahyadri Hills
  • Language: Marathi, Hindi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Highlights

  • Mahalaxmi Temple – revered Hindu shrine
  • New Palace – royal residence and museum
  • Kolhapuri chappals and wrestling legacy

🌿 Natural & Culinary Attractions

  • Rankala Lake – scenic spot for boating and walks
  • Panhala Fort – historic hill fort with views
  • Spicy Kolhapuri cuisine – misal, tambda rassa

🚆 Travel & Infrastructure

  • Rail and road connectivity to Pune, Mumbai, Goa
  • Nearby airport and bus services
