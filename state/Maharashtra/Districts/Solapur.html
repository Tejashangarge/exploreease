Welcome to Solapur

Textile traditions, sacred temples, and vibrant culture

📍 Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Maharashtra
  • Region: Border of Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh
  • Language: Marathi, Kannada, Telugu, Hindi

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Highlights

  • Siddheshwar Temple – iconic lakeside shrine
  • Solapur Chaddar – famous cotton bedspreads
  • Historic textile mills and weaving legacy

🌿 Natural & Religious Attractions

  • Siddheshwar Lake – boating and temple views
  • Bhui-Kot Fort – ancient fortification
  • Nearby pilgrimage sites – Akkalkot, Tuljapur

🚆 Travel & Infrastructure

  • Major railway junction and highway access
  • Bus connectivity to Pune, Hyderabad, and Mumbai

🎯 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Siddheshwar Temple Peaceful temple on the lake with annual fair
Bhui-Kot Fort Historic fort with panoramic city views
Akkalkot Spiritual town dedicated to Swami Samarth
Tuljapur Temple town of Goddess Tulja Bhavani
