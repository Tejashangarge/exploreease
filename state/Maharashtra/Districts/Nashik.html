Welcome to Nashik

Spiritual sanctity meets vineyard serenity

📍 Overview

  • Location: Northern Maharashtra
  • Region: Western Ghats
  • Language: Marathi, Hindi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Highlights

  • Trimbakeshwar Temple – one of the 12 Jyotirlingas
  • Kumbh Mela – held every 12 years on the Godavari banks
  • Ancient links to Ramayana and Hindu mythology

🌿 Natural & Wine Attractions

  • Sula Vineyards – India’s premier wine destination
  • Godavari River ghats – spiritual and scenic
  • Anjneri Hills – believed to be Hanuman’s birthplace

🚆 Travel & Infrastructure

  • Rail and road connectivity to Mumbai and Pune
  • Domestic airport and wine tourism facilities

🎯 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Trimbakeshwar Temple Ancient Shiva temple with sacred water tank
Sula Vineyards Wine tasting, vineyard tours, and events
Panchavati Mythological site with temples and ghats
Anjneri Hills Scenic trekking spot with religious significance
