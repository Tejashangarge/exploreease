Welcome to Ranchi City

Explore the natural beauty, waterfalls, and cultural heritage of Jharkhand

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern India, Jharkhand
  • Region: Chotanagpur Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Nagpuri, Mundari, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birsa Munda Statue and Heritage – honoring tribal freedom fighters
  • Rich tribal culture and festivals like Karma, Sarhul
  • Colonial-era churches and temples reflecting history

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Hundru Falls – stunning waterfall in the midst of greenery
  • Jonha Falls – picturesque waterfall with tranquil surroundings
  • Ranchi Lake – scenic lake perfect for boating and evening strolls

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Wide range of hotels and resorts catering to all budgets
  • Well-connected via Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi Junction railway station, and highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Hundru Falls Mesmerizing waterfall cascading from a height of 98 meters
Jonha Falls Scenic waterfall surrounded by lush forests and serene atmosphere
Tagore Hill Hill offering panoramic views of Ranchi and a peaceful retreat
Ranchi Lake Popular spot for boating, picnics, and evening walks
