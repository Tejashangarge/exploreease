Welcome to Dhanbad

The Coal Capital of India – Explore industry, culture, and nature

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern India, Jharkhand
  • Region: Chotanagpur Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Bengali, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Coal mining heritage and industrial history
  • Temples like Jagannath Mandir and Bholaram Mandir
  • Festivals: Chhath Puja, Durga Puja

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Maithon Dam – serene waters and picnic spots
  • Panchet Hill – trekking and scenic views
  • Dhanbad Lake – tranquil environment for boating

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels catering to all budgets
  • Well-connected via Dhanbad Junction railway station and highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Maithon Dam Man-made reservoir perfect for relaxation and boating
Panchet Hill Hill with trekking trails and panoramic views
Topchanchi Lake Scenic lake surrounded by hills and greenery
Jharia Coal Mines Historic coal mining area showcasing industrial heritage
