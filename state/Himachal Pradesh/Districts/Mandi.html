Welcome to Mandi

Gateway to the Himalayas and the cultural heart of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Mandi District
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Known as the "Varanasi of the Hills" with over 80 ancient temples
  • Rich heritage from the princely state of Mandi
  • Hosts the famous International Mandi Shivratri Fair

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Prashar Lake – a high-altitude lake with a floating island
  • Barot Valley – scenic beauty, trout fishing, and trekking

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guest houses, and homestays available across the region
  • Well-connected via road and nearby Bhuntar Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Prashar Lake Serene lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks and a 13th-century temple
Bhutnath Temple Ancient Shiva temple at the heart of Mandi town
Barot Valley Tranquil valley known for its natural beauty and adventure sports
Rewalsar Lake Holy lake revered by Hindus, Sikhs, and Buddhists alike
