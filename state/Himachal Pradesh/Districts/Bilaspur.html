Welcome to Bilaspur

Explore the lakeside charm and cultural heritage of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Satluj River Basin
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former princely state with deep historical roots
  • Known for the Bhakra Dam and submerged old Bilaspur town
  • Hosts traditional fairs and festivals like Nalwari Mela

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Gobind Sagar Lake – reservoir formed by Bhakra Dam, ideal for water sports
  • Kandrour Bridge – one of Asia’s highest bridges over the Satluj River

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and lakeside resorts available
  • Connected via roadways and nearby railway stations in Kiratpur and Una

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Gobind Sagar Lake Scenic reservoir offering boating, fishing, and panoramic views
Bhakra Dam Engineering marvel and major hydroelectric project on the Satluj River
Kandrour Bridge High-altitude bridge with stunning views of the river valley
Naina Devi Temple Important Shakti Peetha located on a hilltop near Bilaspur
