Welcome to Solan

Explore the Mushroom City of India nestled in the hills of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: South-central Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Shivalik Hills
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Named after Goddess Shoolini Devi, the presiding deity of the region
  • Known for its annual Shoolini Mela and colonial-era architecture
  • Important educational and horticultural hub

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Mohan Shakti Heritage Park – spiritual and cultural complex with scenic views
  • Karol Tibba – popular trekking destination with panoramic vistas

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts, and homestays available across the town
  • Well-connected via Kalka–Shimla railway and national highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mohan Shakti Heritage Park Sprawling spiritual park with temples, sculptures, and gardens
Shoolini Devi Temple Historic temple dedicated to the goddess after whom the town is named
Karol Tibba Hilltop trek offering stunning views and mythological significance
Jatoli Shiv Temple One of the tallest Shiva temples in Asia, known for its architecture
