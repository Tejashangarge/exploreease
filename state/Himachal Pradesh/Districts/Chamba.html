Welcome to Chamba

Discover the timeless beauty and heritage of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northwestern Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Ravi River Valley
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ancient princely state with over 1000 years of history
  • Famous for Chamba Rumal embroidery and miniature paintings
  • Hosts the annual Minjar Fair celebrating harvest and heritage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Khajjiar – often called the “Mini Switzerland of India”
  • Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary – dense deodar forests and rich biodiversity
  • Chamera Lake – scenic reservoir ideal for boating and picnics

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, heritage stays, and forest lodges available
  • Connected via roadways and nearby Pathankot Railway Station

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Khajjiar Meadow surrounded by forests and mountains, perfect for nature lovers
Chamera Lake Man-made lake offering boating and panoramic views
Laxmi Narayan Temple Ancient temple complex showcasing exquisite stone architecture
Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area with trekking trails and wildlife sightings
