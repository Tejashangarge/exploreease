Welcome to Kangra

Explore the spiritual heritage and natural beauty of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Kangra Valley, Dhauladhar Range
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the ancient Kangra Fort, one of India’s oldest forts
  • Center of the famous Kangra miniature painting tradition
  • Spiritual hub with temples like Brajeshwari Devi and Masroor Rock Cut Temples

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Dhauladhar Mountains – snow-capped peaks and trekking trails
  • Pong Dam Lake – a Ramsar wetland site ideal for birdwatching

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, heritage stays, and eco-resorts available
  • Connected via Pathankot Railway Station, Gaggal Airport, and roadways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kangra Fort Historic fort with panoramic views and ancient architecture
Brajeshwari Devi Temple Sacred Shakti Peetha and major pilgrimage site
Masroor Rock Cut Temples Monolithic temples carved from a single rock, dating back to the 8th century
Pong Dam Lake Scenic reservoir and bird sanctuary with boating and nature trails
