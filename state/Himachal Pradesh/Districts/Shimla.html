Welcome to Shimla

Experience the charm of the Queen of Hills in Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Western Himalayas
  • Language: Hindi, English, Pahari

🏰 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former summer capital of British India
  • Colonial architecture and heritage buildings
  • Rich traditions and vibrant local festivals

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Kufri – snowy slopes and adventure activities
  • Jakhoo Hill – highest point in Shimla with panoramic views

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury resorts, heritage hotels, and cozy homestays
  • Well-connected via road, rail (Kalka–Shimla toy train), and nearby airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
The Ridge Open space in the heart of town with stunning mountain views
Jakhoo Temple Temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman atop Jakhoo Hill
Kufri Popular spot for skiing, horse riding, and snow fun
Christ Church Historic neo-Gothic church built in 1857
