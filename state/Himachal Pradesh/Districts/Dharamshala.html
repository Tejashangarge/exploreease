Welcome to Dharamshala

Discover the spiritual serenity and Himalayan beauty of Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Kangra District, Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Dhauladhar Range, Western Himalayas
  • Language: Hindi, Tibetan, English, Pahari

🕉️ Cultural & Spiritual Significance

  • Home to the Tibetan government-in-exile and His Holiness the Dalai Lama
  • Blend of Tibetan and Indian cultures, monasteries, and meditation centers
  • Known for peaceful retreats and Buddhist teachings

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Triund Trek – scenic trail with panoramic views of the Dhauladhar mountains
  • Bhagsu Waterfall – picturesque cascade near Bhagsunag Temple
  • Dal Lake – serene lake surrounded by deodar trees

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Eco-resorts, guesthouses, and spiritual retreats available
  • Connected via Gaggal Airport, Pathankot Railway Station, and roadways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Tsuglagkhang Complex Main temple of the Dalai Lama, featuring a museum and library
Bhagsunag Temple & Waterfall Ancient temple and scenic waterfall popular with trekkers
Triund Popular trekking destination with breathtaking views of the Himalayas
Norbulingka Institute Center for preserving Tibetan art and culture
