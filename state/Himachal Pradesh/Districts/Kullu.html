Welcome to Kullu

Embrace the valley of gods and the gateway to adventure in Himachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Kullu District
  • Language: Hindi, Pahari, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Known for the vibrant Kullu Dussehra festival
  • Rich traditions of handwoven shawls and folk music
  • Spiritual heritage with temples dedicated to Lord Raghunath and other deities

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Great Himalayan National Park – UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Beas River – ideal for rafting and riverside picnics
  • Parvati Valley – gateway to Kasol, Manikaran, and trekking trails

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Resorts, riverside camps, and homestays available
  • Connected via Bhuntar Airport and scenic roadways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Great Himalayan National Park Protected area with alpine meadows, wildlife, and trekking routes
Raghunath Temple Historic temple and center of Kullu Dussehra celebrations
Parvati Valley Scenic valley with spiritual sites and adventure trails
Bijli Mahadev Temple Hilltop temple offering panoramic views and mythological significance
