Welcome to Jodhpur City

Experience the royal heritage and blue-hued charm of Rajasthan

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Marwar
  • Language: Hindi, Marwari, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Mehrangarh Fort – one of India’s largest and most majestic forts
  • Umaid Bhawan Palace – a blend of heritage and luxury
  • Rich traditions of Marwari culture and craftsmanship

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Rao Jodha Desert Rock Park – ecological restoration near the fort
  • Balsamand Lake – peaceful retreat with royal gardens

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, havelis, and modern accommodations
  • Airport, railway junction, and desert highway access

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mehrangarh Fort Massive hilltop fort with panoramic views and museum galleries
Umaid Bhawan Palace Royal residence and luxury hotel with Art Deco design
Jaswant Thada Marble cenotaph with intricate carvings and peaceful gardens
Clock Tower & Sardar Market Vibrant local bazaar for spices, textiles, and handicrafts
