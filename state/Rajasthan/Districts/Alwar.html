Welcome to Alwar City

Explore the gateway to Rajasthan’s hills, heritage, and wilderness

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northeastern Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Mewat
  • Language: Hindi, Rajasthani, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Bala Quila – majestic fort perched on Aravalli hills
  • City Palace – blend of Rajput and Mughal architecture
  • Rich legacy of Alwar princely state and royal patronage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Sariska Tiger Reserve – wildlife sanctuary with tigers, leopards, and ancient temples
  • Siliserh Lake – scenic reservoir with boating and hill views

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, heritage stays, and forest lodges
  • Railway station, highway access, and proximity to Delhi NCR

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Bala Quila Hilltop fort offering panoramic views and historic ramparts
Sariska Tiger Reserve Protected forest with rich biodiversity and ancient ruins
City Palace & Museum Royal complex with courtyards, artifacts, and Mughal gardens
Siliserh Lake Peaceful lake with boating and a heritage palace hotel
