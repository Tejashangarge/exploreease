Welcome to Ajmer City

Experience the spiritual harmony and Mughal-era charm of Rajasthan

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Ajmer Division
  • Language: Hindi, Rajasthani, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ajmer Sharif Dargah – revered Sufi shrine of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti
  • Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra – Indo-Islamic architectural marvel
  • Historic ties with the Delhi Sultanate and Mughal Empire

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ana Sagar Lake – scenic artificial lake with marble pavilions
  • Aravalli hills and nearby Pushkar valley

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, lodges, and religious accommodations
  • Railway junction, road network, and proximity to Kishangarh Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ajmer Sharif Dargah Spiritual center attracting pilgrims from all faiths
Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra Historic mosque built on a Sanskrit college site
Ana Sagar Lake Peaceful lake with gardens and marble pavilions
Taragarh Fort Hilltop fort offering panoramic views of Ajmer
