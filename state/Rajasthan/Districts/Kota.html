Welcome to Kota City

Explore the educational hub and scenic charm of Rajasthan

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Hadoti
  • Language: Hindi, Rajasthani, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Kota Garh (City Palace) – blend of Rajput and Mughal architecture
  • Jagmandir Palace – picturesque structure on Kishore Sagar Lake
  • Rich legacy of miniature paintings and royal heritage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Chambal Garden – riverside park with boating and lush greenery
  • Seven Wonders Park – replicas of global monuments by the lake

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and student accommodations
  • Railway junction, bus terminals, and highway access

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kota Garh Historic palace with museum exhibits and royal artifacts
Jagmandir Palace Elegant palace on Kishore Sagar Lake offering scenic views
Seven Wonders Park Unique park featuring miniatures of world-famous monuments
Chambal Garden Peaceful riverside retreat with boating and nature walks
