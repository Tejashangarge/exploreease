Welcome to Bikaner City

Uncover the desert legacy and camel culture of Rajasthan

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northwestern Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Marwar
  • Language: Hindi, Rajasthani, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Junagarh Fort – richly decorated fort with courtyards and palaces
  • Karni Mata Temple – famous for its sacred rats
  • Royal heritage of the Bikaner princely state

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Gajner Lake – tranquil oasis with migratory birds
  • Desert dunes and camel breeding farms

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, desert camps, and guesthouses
  • Railway station, highway links, and regional airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Junagarh Fort Impressive fort with ornate interiors and historical exhibits
Karni Mata Temple Unique temple known for its revered rats and spiritual aura
National Research Centre on Camel Educational and interactive center dedicated to camel conservation
Gajner Palace Lakeside palace offering serene views and royal architecture
