Welcome to Bharatpur City

Discover the wetlands, wildlife, and royal legacy of Eastern Rajasthan

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Rajasthan, India
  • Region: Braj
  • Language: Hindi, Braj Bhasha, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Lohagarh Fort – symbol of Jat resilience and military strength
  • Royal heritage of Bharatpur rulers and their contributions to conservation
  • Blend of Mughal and Rajput architectural styles

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Keoladeo National Park – UNESCO-listed bird sanctuary with over 370 species
  • Wetlands, woodlands, and seasonal migratory birds

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Eco-lodges, hotels, and forest guesthouses
  • Railway station, highway access, and proximity to Agra and Delhi

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Keoladeo National Park World-renowned bird sanctuary ideal for nature walks and photography
Lohagarh Fort Sturdy fort with historical significance and unique architecture
Government Museum Artifacts and exhibits showcasing Bharatpur’s royal and natural history
Ganga Mandir Beautiful temple dedicated to Goddess Ganga with intricate carvings
