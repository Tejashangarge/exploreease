Welcome to Benaulim

Experience the tranquil beaches, rich heritage, and warm hospitality of South Goa

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: South Goa, India
  • Region: Konkan
  • Language: Konkani, English, Marathi, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Historic fishing village with Portuguese influence
  • St. John the Baptist Church – 16th-century architecture
  • Local festivals and traditional Goan cuisine

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Benaulim Beach – peaceful shores and dolphin spotting
  • Sal River – lush backwaters and birdwatching

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Beachside resorts, guesthouses, and eco-stays
  • Accessible via Margao railway station and Dabolim airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Benaulim Beach Serene beach ideal for relaxation and water activities
St. John the Baptist Church Historic church with colonial architecture and scenic surroundings
Sal River Backwaters Peaceful river cruises and nature trails
Local Market Handicrafts, spices, and Goan delicacies
