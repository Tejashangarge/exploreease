Welcome to Calangute

Experience the vibrant beach life and coastal charm of North Goa

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North Goa, India
  • Region: Konkan Coast
  • Language: Konkani, English, Marathi, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Popular since the hippie era of the 1960s
  • Blend of Goan, Portuguese, and global beach cultures
  • Hosts vibrant festivals and beach carnivals

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Calangute Beach – golden sands and lively atmosphere
  • Sunsets, palm-lined shores, and water sports

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Beach resorts, shacks, hotels, and nightlife hubs
  • Well-connected by road and close to Thivim railway station

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Calangute Beach Goa’s most famous beach with water sports and nightlife
Baga Beach Adjacent beach known for parties and beach clubs
St. Alex Church Beautiful white church with colonial architecture
Tibetan Market Colorful stalls with handicrafts, jewelry, and souvenirs
