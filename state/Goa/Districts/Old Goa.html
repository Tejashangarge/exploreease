Welcome to Old Goa

Step into the sacred legacy of Goa’s colonial past and spiritual grandeur

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North Goa, India
  • Region: Konkan
  • Language: Konkani, English, Marathi, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former capital of Portuguese India and a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Renowned for grand cathedrals and baroque churches
  • Spiritual center for Christian pilgrims and heritage lovers

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Mandovi River banks and lush countryside views
  • Nearby islands and serene village landscapes

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage guesthouses, eco-resorts, and guided heritage walks
  • Accessible from Panaji and well-connected by road

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Basilica of Bom Jesus Houses the relics of St. Francis Xavier, a UNESCO World Heritage site
Se Cathedral One of the largest churches in Asia, dedicated to St. Catherine
Church of St. Francis of Assisi Baroque-style church with intricate interiors and a museum
Archaeological Museum Showcases Portuguese artifacts, sculptures, and colonial history
