Welcome to Mapusa City

Uncover the vibrant markets and cultural heritage of North Goa

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North Goa, India
  • Region: Konkan Coast
  • Language: Konkani, English, Marathi, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Traditional Goan town with Portuguese colonial influences
  • Famous for its bustling Friday market
  • Gateway to nearby heritage villages and churches

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Close to beaches like Anjuna and Vagator
  • Surrounded by lush hills and countryside

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Guesthouses, boutique hotels, and homestays
  • Well-connected by road and nearby Thivim railway station

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mapusa Friday Market Colorful weekly market with local produce, crafts, and spices
St. Jerome Church Historic church with annual feast celebrations
Moira Village Charming village known for its heritage homes and serene ambiance
Anjuna Beach Nearby beach famous for its flea market and nightlife
