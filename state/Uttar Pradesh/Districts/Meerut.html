Welcome to Meerut City

Explore the cradle of India’s first war of independence and its vibrant cultural legacy

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Region: Upper Ganges Plain
  • Language: Hindi, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Augarnath Temple – associated with the 1857 revolt
  • St. John's Church – colonial-era Anglican church
  • Gandhi Bagh – historic garden with musical fountain

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Suraj Kund Park – serene green space with a pond
  • Ecological Park – nature trails and picnic zones

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, lodges, and heritage stays
  • Well-connected by road, rail, and nearby airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Augarnath Temple Historic temple linked to the 1857 uprising
St. John's Church British-era church with Gothic architecture
Gandhi Bagh Peaceful garden with evening light and sound shows
Suraj Kund Park Popular for morning walks and family outings
