Welcome to Agra City

Step into the legacy of love and Mughal grandeur on the banks of the Yamuna

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Region: Braj
  • Language: Hindi, Urdu, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Taj Mahal – world-famous symbol of love and UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Agra Fort – red sandstone fortress with palaces and mosques
  • Itmad-ud-Daulah – often called the “Baby Taj” for its intricate marble work

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Mehtab Bagh – riverside garden offering a perfect view of the Taj at sunset
  • Yamuna Riverfront – peaceful walks and boat rides

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury hotels, heritage stays, and budget lodges
  • International airport (nearby), railway junction, and expressway connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Taj Mahal Marble mausoleum built by Shah Jahan, renowned for its beauty and symmetry
Agra Fort Massive Mughal fort with royal halls, gardens, and a view of the Taj
Itmad-ud-Daulah Delicate tomb with pietra dura inlay, precursor to the Taj Mahal
Fatehpur Sikri Historic Mughal capital with grand palaces and the Buland Darwaza
