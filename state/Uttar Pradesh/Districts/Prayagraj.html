Welcome to Prayagraj City

Witness the spiritual confluence and cultural depth of India’s sacred Sangam city

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southern Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Region: Awadh-Bundelkhand border
  • Language: Hindi, Urdu, Sanskrit, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Triveni Sangam – sacred confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and mythical Saraswati rivers
  • Kumbh Mela – world’s largest religious gathering held every 12 years
  • Anand Bhavan – ancestral home of the Nehru-Gandhi family

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Yamuna Riverfront – serene ghats and boat rides
  • Chandrashekhar Azad Park – lush green space with historical monuments

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Spiritual lodges, heritage hotels, and modern accommodations
  • Airport, major railway junction, and highway connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Triveni Sangam Holy confluence where pilgrims take ritual baths and perform religious rites
Anand Bhavan Historic residence turned museum chronicling India’s freedom movement
Allahabad Fort Mughal-era fort built by Akbar near the Sangam, now under army control
Khusro Bagh Mughal garden with tombs of royal family members and intricate carvings
