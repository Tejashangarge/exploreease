Welcome to Varanasi City

Immerse yourself in the spiritual soul of India along the sacred Ganges

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Region: Purvanchal
  • Language: Hindi, Bhojpuri, Sanskrit, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple – one of the holiest Shiva temples
  • Sarnath – birthplace of Buddhism where Buddha gave his first sermon
  • Dashashwamedh Ghat – vibrant riverside rituals and evening Ganga Aarti

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ganges River – boat rides at sunrise and spiritual cleansing
  • Assi Ghat – peaceful spot for yoga and morning prayers

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Spiritual retreats, heritage hotels, and budget guesthouses
  • Airport, railway station, and waterway connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kashi Vishwanath Temple Ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, central to Hindu pilgrimage
Sarnath Historic site with stupas and museums marking the origin of Buddhism
Dashashwamedh Ghat Main ghat known for its dramatic evening Aarti and spiritual ambiance
Ramnagar Fort 18th-century fort and museum showcasing royal artifacts and vintage vehicles
