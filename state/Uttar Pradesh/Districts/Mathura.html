Welcome to Mathura City

Explore the birthplace of Lord Krishna and the heart of Braj Bhoomi

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Region: Braj region
  • Language: Hindi, Braj Bhasha, Sanskrit, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi – revered site believed to be Lord Krishna’s birthplace
  • Vibrant Holi celebrations – especially in nearby Barsana and Nandgaon
  • Rich legacy of temples, folklore, and devotional music

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Yamuna Ghats – scenic riverbanks with evening aartis and boat rides
  • Vishram Ghat – peaceful spot where Krishna is said to have rested after slaying Kansa

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Dharmshalas, heritage guesthouses, and modern hotels
  • Railway station, highway access, and proximity to Agra and Delhi

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple complex marking the birthplace of Lord Krishna
Dwarkadhish Temple Architecturally rich temple dedicated to Krishna as King of Dwarka
Vishram Ghat Spiritual bathing ghat on the Yamuna with evening rituals and boat rides
Gita Mandir Temple known for its inscriptions of the entire Bhagavad Gita
