Welcome to Katni

Explore the marble capital and riverfront beauty of Madhya Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Eastern Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Mahakoshal
  • Language: Hindi, Bagheli, English

🏛️ Cultural & Economic Significance

  • Known for its marble and limestone industries
  • Important railway junction connecting central India
  • Blend of tribal, rural, and urban cultures

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Katni River – scenic river flowing through the city
  • Bheraghat-style marble formations in nearby quarries
  • Surrounding forests and hills ideal for nature walks

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and business stays available
  • Connected via Katni Junction and road from Jabalpur and Satna

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Roopnath Temple Ancient rock-cut temple with inscriptions and natural spring
Vijayraghavgarh Fort Historic fort with scenic views and cultural significance
Katni Riverfront Peaceful riverside area ideal for walks and local gatherings
Jhinjhari Waterfall Seasonal waterfall surrounded by greenery and rocky terrain
