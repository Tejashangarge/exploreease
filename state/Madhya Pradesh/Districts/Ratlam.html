Welcome to Ratlam

Explore the spice-scented streets and railway legacy of Madhya Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Western Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Malwa Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Malwi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded by Raja Ratan Singh in the 17th century
  • Major railway junction and commercial hub
  • Known for Ratlami Sev, spices, and jewelry markets

🌄 Local Attractions

  • Jama Masjid – historic mosque with Indo-Islamic architecture
  • Cactus Garden – unique botanical garden with rare species
  • Kalika Mata Mandir – revered temple on a hilltop

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and business stays available
  • Connected via Ratlam Junction and road from Indore and Ujjain

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Jama Masjid Historic mosque with intricate carvings and peaceful ambiance
Kalika Mata Temple Hilltop temple offering panoramic views and spiritual serenity
Cactus Garden Botanical garden with diverse cactus species and desert flora
Ratlami Sev Market Famous for spicy snacks and bustling local bazaars
