Welcome to Sagar

Explore the lakeside charm and cultural heritage of Madhya Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Northeastern Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Vindhya Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Bundeli, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded in the 18th century by the Maratha rulers
  • Known for Dr. Hari Singh Gour University, one of India’s oldest universities
  • Blend of academic, cultural, and religious traditions

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Sagar Lake – central freshwater lake ideal for boating and leisure
  • Surrounding hills and gardens offering scenic views

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and university accommodations available
  • Connected via Sagar Railway Station and road from Bhopal and Jabalpur

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Sagar Lake Historic lake surrounded by temples and gardens
Dr. Hari Singh Gour University Prestigious educational institution with colonial architecture
Rahatgarh Waterfall Scenic waterfall located near Sagar, ideal for nature lovers
Garhphra Mountains Hill range offering trekking and panoramic views
