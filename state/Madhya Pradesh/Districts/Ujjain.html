Welcome to Ujjain

Experience the spiritual soul of Madhya Pradesh on the banks of the Shipra River

📍 Overview

  • Location: Western Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Malwa Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Sanskrit, English

🛕 Cultural & Religious Significance

  • One of the seven sacred cities (Sapta Puri) in Hinduism
  • Hosts the Kumbh Mela every 12 years, attracting millions of pilgrims
  • Home to Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas

🌄 Spiritual Attractions

  • Shipra River Ghats – site of daily aartis and holy dips
  • Sandipani Ashram – ancient gurukul where Lord Krishna studied
  • Kal Bhairav Temple – unique temple where liquor is offered as prasad

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Pilgrim lodges, hotels, and dharamshalas available
  • Connected via Ujjain Junction and road from Indore and Bhopal

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mahakaleshwar Temple One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, known for its Bhasma Aarti
Ram Ghat Prominent ghat on the Shipra River, site of Kumbh rituals
Kal Bhairav Temple Temple where devotees offer liquor to the deity
Sandipani Ashram Ancient learning center associated with Lord Krishna
