Welcome to Gwalior

Explore the fortress city of Madhya Pradesh and its royal legacy

📍 Overview

  • Location: Northern Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Chambal River Basin
  • Language: Hindi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ruled by Tomars, Mughals, Marathas, and Scindias
  • Birthplace of Tansen, legendary musician of Akbar’s court
  • Known for Gwalior Gharana of Hindustani classical music

🌄 Architectural Attractions

  • Gwalior Fort – majestic hilltop fort with palaces and temples
  • Jai Vilas Palace – opulent Scindia residence with museum
  • Sas Bahu Temples – intricately carved twin temples

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, budget stays, and guided tours available
  • Connected via Gwalior Junction and Rajmata Vijaya Raje Scindia Airport

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Gwalior Fort Historic fort with Man Singh Palace, temples, and panoramic views
Jai Vilas Palace Royal residence featuring world’s largest chandeliers and museum
Tomb of Tansen Memorial to the legendary musician, part of annual music festival
Gopachal Parvat Rock-cut Jain sculptures carved into the hillside
