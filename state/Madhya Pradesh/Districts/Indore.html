Welcome to Indore

Discover the commercial capital of Madhya Pradesh and its vibrant culture

📍 Overview

  • Location: Western Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Region: Malwa Plateau
  • Language: Hindi, Malwi, English

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded in the 18th century under the Holkar dynasty
  • Known for Rajwada Palace and Lal Bagh heritage
  • Hosts vibrant festivals and a thriving arts scene

🌆 Urban Attractions

  • Chappan Dukan – iconic food street with Indore’s famous snacks
  • Rajwada Palace – blend of Maratha, Mughal, and French architecture
  • Pipliyapala Regional Park – lakeside park with boating and gardens

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury hotels, business stays, and budget accommodations available
  • Connected via Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport and Indore Junction

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Rajwada Palace Historic palace in the heart of the city with cultural exhibits
Chappan Dukan Famous food street offering Indore’s best street snacks
Lal Bagh Palace Elegant palace showcasing Holkar dynasty’s grandeur
Pipliyapala Regional Park Urban park with boating, fountains, and landscaped gardens
