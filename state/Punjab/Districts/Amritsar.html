Welcome to Amritsar City

Discover the spiritual heart of Punjab and its vibrant heritage

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northwestern Punjab, India
  • Region: Majha
  • Language: Punjabi, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Golden Temple – the holiest shrine of Sikhism
  • Jallianwala Bagh – memorial of India's freedom struggle
  • Rich legacy of Maharaja Ranjit Singh and Sikh Empire

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ram Bagh Garden – historic landscaped park
  • Serene lakes and green spaces around the city

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Wide range of hotels and dharamshalas
  • International airport, rail hub, and highway access

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Golden Temple Iconic Sikh shrine with shimmering architecture and spiritual ambiance
Jallianwala Bagh Historic garden and memorial of the 1919 massacre
Partition Museum Emotional journey through India's partition history
Wagah Border Daily flag ceremony showcasing patriotic fervor
