Welcome to Ludhiana City

Experience the industrial pulse and cultural charm of Punjab

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Punjab, India
  • Region: Malwa
  • Language: Punjabi, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Blend of Sikh, Hindu, and colonial heritage
  • Historic Gurudwaras and temples across the city
  • Legacy of textile and industrial growth since British era

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Nehru Rose Garden – lush greenery and floral beauty
  • Pavilion Mall rooftop views and urban parks

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury hotels, business stays, and budget lodges
  • Railway junction, domestic airport, and highway links
