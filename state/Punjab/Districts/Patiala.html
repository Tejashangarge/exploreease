Welcome to Patiala City

Explore royal heritage, vibrant culture, and architectural splendor of Punjab

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Punjab, India
  • Region: Malwa
  • Language: Punjabi, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded in 1763 by Baba Ala Singh, a princely state with rich royal legacy
  • Known for Patiala Peg, Patiala Salwar, and classical music traditions
  • Blend of Sikh, Mughal, and Rajput architectural styles

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Baradari Gardens – lush royal gardens with colonial charm
  • Bird-watching and nature walks in nearby countryside

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, modern stays, and traditional guesthouses
  • Well-connected by rail, road, and nearby airports

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Qila Mubarak Historic fort complex showcasing royal Patiala architecture
Sheesh Mahal Palace of mirrors with murals, museum, and suspension bridge
Baradari Gardens Beautiful garden with colonial buildings and floral landscapes
Gurudwara Dukh Nivaran Sahib Spiritual site believed to have healing powers
