Welcome to Bathinda City

Explore the ancient forts, thermal lakes, and vibrant culture of Punjab

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Punjab, India
  • Region: Malwa
  • Language: Punjabi, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • One of the oldest cities in Punjab with links to the Kushan and Gupta empires
  • Historic forts and temples reflecting centuries of heritage
  • Known for its contribution to agriculture and thermal energy

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Thermal Lake – unique natural hot water spring
  • Rose Garden – peaceful retreat with floral beauty

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts, and dharamshalas for varied budgets
  • Railway junction, road network, and nearby airport access

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Qila Mubarak Ancient fort believed to be over 1,900 years old
Thermal Lake Natural hot water lake with therapeutic properties
Rose Garden Beautiful park with walking paths and seasonal blooms
Chetak Park Family-friendly amusement park with rides and greenery
