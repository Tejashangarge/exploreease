Welcome to Hoshiarpur City

Discover the foothills of Shivalik, rich heritage, and spiritual serenity of Punjab

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northeastern Punjab, India
  • Region: Doaba
  • Language: Punjabi, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Known as the “Land of Saints” for its spiritual legacy
  • Historic temples, Gurudwaras, and archaeological sites
  • Rich tradition in classical music and craftsmanship

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Shivalik foothills – scenic drives and trekking trails
  • Forests and nature reserves with diverse flora and fauna

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and eco-stays available
  • Connected by road and rail; nearest airport in Adampur

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Takhni-Rehmapur Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area ideal for nature lovers and birdwatchers
Sheesh Mahal Mandir Historic temple with intricate mirror work and spiritual ambiance
Gurudwara Garna Sahib Peaceful Sikh shrine nestled in natural surroundings
Archaeological Museum Showcases ancient artifacts and regional history
