Welcome to Mysuru District

Discover the royal heritage, vibrant culture, and natural charm of Karnataka

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southern Karnataka, India
  • Region: Mysore Division
  • Language: Kannada, English, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Mysore Palace – a symbol of royal grandeur and architectural brilliance
  • Legacy of the Wodeyar dynasty and Tipu Sultan
  • Rich traditions in classical music, dance, and Dasara festivities

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Chamundi Hills – panoramic views and sacred temples
  • Karanji Lake and Brindavan Gardens – serene escapes with lush greenery

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Wide range of hotels, homestays, and heritage resorts
  • Well-connected by rail, road, and nearby airports

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mysore Palace Iconic royal residence with Indo-Saracenic architecture
Chamundi Hill Spiritual site with the Chamundeshwari Temple and scenic views
Brindavan Gardens Famous for musical fountains and landscaped beauty
St. Philomena’s Church Neo-Gothic cathedral with stunning stained glass windows
