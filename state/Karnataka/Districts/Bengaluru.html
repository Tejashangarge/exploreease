Welcome to Bengaluru District

Explore the vibrant tech hub, lush gardens, and cultural landmarks of Karnataka’s capital city

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Karnataka, India
  • Region: Bengaluru Division
  • Language: Kannada, English, Hindi

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Blend of modern innovation and historical architecture
  • Home to art galleries, music festivals, and literary events
  • Legacy of Kempegowda and colonial-era landmarks

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Lalbagh Botanical Garden – historic garden with rare plant species
  • Cubbon Park – green oasis in the heart of the city

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury hotels, boutique stays, and budget accommodations
  • Well-connected by road, rail, and Kempegowda International Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Lalbagh Botanical Garden Historic garden with glasshouse and annual flower shows
Bangalore Palace Royal residence inspired by Tudor architecture
ISKCON Temple Spiritual center with grand architecture and cultural programs
Vidhana Soudha Iconic government building showcasing neo-Dravidian style
