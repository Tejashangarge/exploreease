Welcome to Mangaluru District

Experience the coastal beauty, cultural diversity, and culinary delights of Karnataka

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Karnataka, India
  • Region: Coastal Karnataka
  • Language: Kannada, Tulu, Konkani, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Rich heritage of Tulu Nadu and ancient temples
  • Influence of Portuguese, Arab, and British traders
  • Celebrations like Mangaluru Dasara and Yakshagana performances

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Panambur Beach – popular for sunsets and water sports
  • Pilikula Nisargadhama – eco-education and nature park

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Seaside resorts, city hotels, and homestays
  • Well-connected by road, rail, and Mangaluru International Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Panambur Beach Clean and lively beach with activities and food stalls
Kadri Manjunath Temple Ancient temple with bronze statues and scenic surroundings
Pilikula Nisargadhama Nature park with botanical gardens, zoo, and heritage village
St. Aloysius Chapel Historic chapel known for its stunning frescoes and architecture
