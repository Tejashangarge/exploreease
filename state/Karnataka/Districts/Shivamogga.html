Welcome to Shivamogga District

Immerse yourself in the verdant beauty and cultural heritage of Karnataka’s Malnad region

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Western Karnataka, India
  • Region: Malnad
  • Language: Kannada, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birthplace of renowned poet Kuvempu and center of Kannada literature
  • Rich traditions in folk art, Yakshagana, and temple architecture
  • Historic sites linked to the Keladi Nayakas and Vijayanagara Empire

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Jog Falls – one of India’s highest and most spectacular waterfalls
  • Agumbe – known as the “Cherrapunji of the South” for its rainforest and sunsets

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Eco-resorts, forest lodges, and city hotels
  • Well-connected by road and rail; nearest airport in Mangaluru or Bengaluru

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Jog Falls Majestic segmented waterfall on the Sharavathi River
Agumbe Rainforest village with sunset viewpoints and biodiversity research center
Kavaledurga Fort Hilltop fort with panoramic views and historical ruins
Sacred Heart Cathedral Prominent church in Shivamogga with neo-Gothic architecture
