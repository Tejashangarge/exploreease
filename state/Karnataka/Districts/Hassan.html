Welcome to Hassan District

Uncover the architectural marvels and serene landscapes of Karnataka

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: South-central Karnataka, India
  • Region: Malnad and Bayaluseeme
  • Language: Kannada, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Seat of the Hoysala Empire with rich temple heritage
  • Home to exquisite Hoysala architecture and sculpture
  • Celebrations of local festivals and traditional crafts

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Bisle Ghat – lush rainforest and breathtaking viewpoints
  • Hemavathi Reservoir – tranquil waters and birdwatching

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels, budget stays, and eco-lodges
  • Connected by road and rail; nearest airport in Mangaluru or Bengaluru

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Belur Temple Chennakesava Temple showcasing intricate Hoysala carvings
Halebidu Ruins of the Hoysaleswara Temple with stunning sculptures
Shravanabelagola Jain pilgrimage site with a towering statue of Gommateshwara
Bisle View Point Scenic spot offering panoramic views of Western Ghats
