Welcome to Udupi District

Experience the coastal charm, spiritual sanctity, and culinary delights of Karnataka’s temple town

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Karnataka, India
  • Region: Coastal Karnataka
  • Language: Kannada, Tulu, Konkani, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Famous for the Udupi Sri Krishna Temple and Ashta Mathas
  • Birthplace of the Udupi cuisine and Dvaita philosophy
  • Rich traditions in temple festivals and classical music

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Malpe Beach – golden sands and water sports
  • Kudlu Falls – hidden gem in the Western Ghats

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Beach resorts, pilgrim lodges, and homestays
  • Well-connected by road, rail, and nearby Mangaluru Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Udupi Sri Krishna Temple Historic temple known for its unique Kanakana Kindi and rituals
Malpe Beach Popular beach with boating to St. Mary’s Island and adventure sports
Kudlu Falls Scenic waterfall nestled in the lush forests of the Western Ghats
Manipal University town with museums, cultural centers, and vibrant student life
