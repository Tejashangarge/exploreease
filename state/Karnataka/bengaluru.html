Bengaluru – Silicon Valley of India

Bengaluru, formerly Bangalore, is Karnataka's capital and India's premier technology hub, blending IT innovation, pleasant climate, vibrant nightlife and rich cultural heritage amidst lush green spaces.

Tech • Gardens • Pubs • Cubbon Park

Major attractions & experiences

Visitors explore Cubbon Park greenery, majestic Bangalore Palace, tech museums like Visvesvaraya Industrial, Lalbagh Botanical Gardens, and vibrant markets like Commercial Street and KR Market.

Iconic spots include Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace, Bull Temple, ISKCON Temple, plus nightlife in Indiranagar, MG Road pubs and nearby hill stations like Nandi Hills.

Cubbon Park
300-acre lung of the city with museums, gardens, walking paths and British-era architecture.
Bangalore Palace
Tudor-style royal palace modeled after Windsor Castle with audio tours and musical fountains.
Lalbagh Botanical Garden
240-acre garden with glasshouse, lake, 1000+ plant species and famous flower shows.
Bannerghatta National Park
Wildlife sanctuary with safaris, zoo, butterfly park and trekking trails just 22km away.

City overview & history

Bengaluru is India's third most populous city and a global technology center, serving as Karnataka's capital with a thriving startup ecosystem and aerospace industry.

Founded in the 16th century by Kempe Gowda, the city evolved from a mud fort into British Cantonment, later becoming India's IT capital during the 1990s software boom.

Known as the "Pub Capital of India" for nightlife and "Garden City" for its greenery, plus major contributions to India's space and defense sectors.

Quick facts

  • Region: Deccan Plateau, southern Karnataka.
  • Key identity: IT capital, startup hub and aerospace center.
  • Population: Over 13 million in metro area.
  • Languages: Kannada, English, Hindi and others.

Climate & best time to visit

Bengaluru enjoys a moderate tropical savanna climate with pleasant temperatures year-round due to its high elevation, though monsoon brings heavy rains.

Best visiting months are October to March when weather is cool and comfortable for outdoor exploration and festivals.

Summer (March-May) sees temperatures up to 35°C, while monsoon (June-September) delivers consistent rainfall averaging 1000mm annually.

Best season: Oct – Mar.
Summer highs: 33–36°C.
Monsoon: Jun – Sep.

Culture, food & lifestyle

Bengaluru thrives as a cosmopolitan melting pot with Kannada culture, international cuisine, rock music scene, pub culture and festivals like Ugadi, Dasara and Christmas celebrations.

Famous for cosmopolitan food scene from dosas, filter coffee to global cuisines, plus iconic street eats at VV Puram food street and fine dining in Koramangala.

Silicon Valley vibe with tech parks, startup cafes, live music venues, malls and India's busiest airport serving global connectivity.

Why Bengaluru stands out

  • India's undisputed technology and startup capital.
  • Perfect year-round climate attracting professionals worldwide.
  • Unique mix of green spaces, nightlife, history and innovation hubs.

Travel tips for visitors

Allocate 3-4 days for central attractions, parks, palaces plus nightlife; use metro, Uber or BMTC buses; extend for day trips to Mysore, Coorg or Shivanasamudra Falls.

  • Year-round pleasant weather; evenings cool even in summer; carry light jacket.
  • Pubs and nightlife thrive post-8 PM; many spots require reservations on weekends.
  • Traffic heavy during peak hours; plan early mornings for gardens/parks, evenings for markets.
