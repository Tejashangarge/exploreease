Welcome to Gurugram City

Discover India's corporate capital and modern lifestyle hub

🏙️ Overview

  • Location: Haryana, India (Delhi NCR)
  • Region: Gurugram District
  • Language: Hindi, English, Haryanvi, Punjabi

🏢 Corporate & Cultural Significance

  • India's largest corporate hub with 500+ Fortune 500 companies
  • Home to Cyber City - India's Silicon Valley
  • Kingdom of Dreams - India's only live entertainment theatre
  • Perfect blend of modern skyscrapers and ancient heritage

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Sultanpur National Park – bird sanctuary
  • Damdama Lake – boating and adventure sports
  • Aravalli Biodiversity Park – nature trails
  • Leisure Valley Park – 8km green corridor

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent metro and highway connectivity to Delhi Airport
  • World-class hotels, malls and entertainment zones
  • Indira Gandhi International Airport (20 mins away)
