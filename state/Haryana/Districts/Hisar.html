Welcome to Hisar City

Discover Haryana's historical heartland and archaeological treasures

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Western Haryana, India
  • Region: Hisar District
  • Language: Hindi, Haryanvi, Punjabi

🏰 Historical & Archaeological Significance

  • Home to Rakhigarhi - largest Harappan site in India
  • Firoz Shah Palace Complex - 14th century Tughlaq architecture
  • Agroha Dham - ancient Jain & Hindu pilgrimage site
  • Rich Indus Valley Civilization heritage

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Blue Bird Lake – scenic man-made reservoir
  • Hisar Deer Park – wildlife and nature spot
  • Town Park – popular green space

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Well-connected by NH-9 and rail network
  • Budget hotels and dharamshalas available
  • Nearby Agroha and Hansi tourist circuits

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Rakhigarhi Largest Indus Valley site (5000+ years old)
Firoz Shah Palace Tughlaq-era fort complex
Agroha Dham Major religious & archaeological site
Blue Bird Lake Popular picnic and boating spot
