Welcome to Ambala City

Discover the industrial hub and historical charm of Haryana

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Haryana, India
  • Region: Ambala District
  • Language: Hindi, Punjabi, Haryanvi, English

🏭 Industrial & Historical Significance

  • India's tool & scientific instrument capital
  • British-era military cantonment (1840s)
  • Home to major PSUs and defense units
  • Rich Sikh and Hindu heritage sites

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Tangri River – scenic riverside walks
  • Green parks and gardens
  • Nearby Badkhal Lake for picnics

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent road connectivity (NH-44)
  • Major railway junction (Ambala Cantt)
  • Hotels for all budgets available

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
St. Paul's Church 1840s colonial architecture
Sheikh Chilli's Tomb Mughal-era mausoleum
Ambala Clock Tower Historic market landmark
Raghunath Mandir Ancient temple complex
