Welcome to Panchkula City

Gateway to Shivaliks & Green Paradise

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Haryana, India
  • Region: Panchkula District
  • Language: Hindi, Punjabi, English

🏰 Historical & Archaeological Significance

  • Morni Hills - Haryana's only hill station
  • Pinjore Gardens - 17th century Mughal gardens
  • Mansa Devi Temple - one of 52 Shakti Peeths
  • Nada Sahib Gurudwara - Sikh pilgrimage site

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Morni Hills – scenic hill station with lakes
  • Chattbir Zoo – wildlife safari experience
  • Cactus Garden – Asia's largest outdoor cactus garden

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Part of Chandigarh Tricity (5 mins from Chandigarh)
  • Excellent hotels, resorts and luxury stays
  • Near Kalka, Shimla and hill station circuits
