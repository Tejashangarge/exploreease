Welcome to Faridabad City

Explore the industrial powerhouse and cultural heritage of Haryana

🏭 Overview

  • Location: Southern Haryana, India (Delhi NCR)
  • Region: Faridabad District
  • Language: Hindi, Haryanvi, Punjabi, English

🏢 Industrial & Cultural Significance

  • Major industrial hub of Haryana with 1000+ factories
  • Part of Delhi National Capital Region (NCR)
  • Rich historical sites and modern infrastructure
  • Host of famous Surajkund International Crafts Mela

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Badkhal Lake – scenic reservoir with boating
  • Surajkund Lake – ancient reservoir with temple ruins
  • Aravalli hills for nature walks and picnics

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent connectivity to Delhi (Mathura Road)
  • Multiple railway stations and metro connectivity
  • Wide range of hotels and budget accommodations

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Surajkund Lake 10th century reservoir, crafts mela venue
Badkhal Lake Popular picnic spot with water sports
Baba Farid Tomb Historical Sufi shrine in Old Faridabad
Raja Nahar Singh Palace 19th century Ballabgarh fort palace
