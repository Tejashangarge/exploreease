Welcome to Panipat City

City of Three Historic Battles

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Haryana, India
  • Region: Panipat District
  • Language: Hindi, Haryanvi, Punjabi

🏰 Historical & Archaeological Significance

  • Site of 3 decisive battles (1526, 1556, 1761)
  • Panipat Museum - artifacts from historic battles
  • Ibrahim Lodi's Tomb - First Battle memorial
  • Textile capital famous for handloom industry

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Kala Amb Park – popular green space
  • Panipat Refinery Township Park – recreational area
  • Local gardens and picnic spots

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Strategic location on NH-44 (Grand Trunk Road)
  • Budget hotels and good connectivity
  • Near Kurukshetra, Karnal tourist circuits

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Panipat Museum Battle artifacts and historical exhibits
Kabuli Bagh Mosque Built by Babur after First Battle victory
Ibrahim Lodi Tomb Memorial of First Battle casualty
Devi Temple Ancient Hindu worship site
