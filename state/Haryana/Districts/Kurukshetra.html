Dharamkshetra - Land of Bhagavad Gita

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Haryana, India
  • Region: Kurukshetra District
  • Language: Hindi, Haryanvi, Punjabi

🏰 Historical & Archaeological Significance

  • Site of Mahabharata war - "Land of Bhagavad Gita"
  • Brahma Sarovar - sacred tank created by Lord Brahma
  • Jyotisar - where Krishna delivered Bhagavad Gita
  • One of 48 Kos Parikrama pilgrimage circuit

🌳 Natural Attractions

  • Brahma Sarovar – massive sacred water reservoir
  • Sannihit Sarovar – confluence of all holy rivers
  • Bhishma Kund – ancient spring from Mahabharata

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent connectivity via NH-44 (Grand Trunk Road)
  • Hotels, dharamshalas and pilgrim accommodations
  • Nearby Karnal, Panipat and Pehowa circuits

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Brahma Sarovar Sacred tank for spiritual cleansing
Jyotisar Bhagavad Gita preaching site
Bhadrakali Temple One of 51 Shakti Peeths
Krishna Museum Mahabharata artifacts and exhibits
