Welcome to Rajahmundry City

Discover the cultural capital of Andhra Pradesh along the banks of the Godavari River

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Coastal Andhra
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Known as the birthplace of Telugu language and literature
  • Historic temples and colonial-era architecture
  • Rich legacy of poets, artists, and scholars

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Godavari River – scenic riverfront with boating and festivals
  • Papi Hills – lush hills accessible via river cruise

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, resorts, and transport facilities available
  • Connected via Rajahmundry Airport, railway station, and highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Godavari Riverfront Beautiful riverside promenade with cultural events and boat rides
Papi Hills Scenic hill range accessible by boat, ideal for nature lovers
ISKCON Rajahmundry Peaceful spiritual center with vibrant architecture
Dowleswaram Barrage Engineering marvel on the Godavari River
