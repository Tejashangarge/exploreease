Welcome to Anantapur City

Discover the historic forts, spiritual sites, and serene landscapes of Andhra Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Rayalaseema
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Lepakshi Temple – renowned for its hanging pillar and Vijayanagara architecture
  • Penukonda Fort – historic fort with royal ruins and scenic views
  • Thimmamma Marrimanu – sacred banyan tree with cultural legends

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Bugga Ramalingeswara Swami Temple – peaceful temple with natural springs
  • Rayadurg Fort – hilltop fort with panoramic views and ancient structures

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Comfortable accommodations and local eateries available
  • Connected via Anantapur Railway Station and nearby Bengaluru Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Lepakshi Temple Architectural marvel with intricate carvings and mythological significance
Penukonda Fort Historic fort showcasing royal heritage and scenic landscapes
Thimmamma Marrimanu World’s largest banyan tree with spiritual and ecological value
