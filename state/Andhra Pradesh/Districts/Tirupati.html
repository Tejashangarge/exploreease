Welcome to Tirupati City

Discover the divine, cultural, and scenic treasures of Andhra Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Rayalaseema
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Sri Venkateswara Temple – one of the most revered Hindu pilgrimage sites
  • Rich heritage dating back to ancient dynasties like Pallavas and Cholas
  • Dravidian architecture and sacred traditions

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Tirumala Hills – lush greenery and spiritual ambiance
  • Talakona Waterfalls – highest waterfall in Andhra Pradesh

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Wide range of accommodations from budget lodges to luxury hotels
  • Well-connected via Tirupati International Airport, railway station, and highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Sri Venkateswara Temple Iconic temple atop Tirumala Hills, known for its spiritual aura
Talakona Waterfalls Scenic waterfall surrounded by dense forest and trekking trails
Chandragiri Fort Historic fort with panoramic views and royal architecture
Kapila Theertham Temple and waterfall nestled at the base of Tirumala Hills
