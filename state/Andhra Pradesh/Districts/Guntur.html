Welcome to Guntur City

Explore the cultural richness, historical landmarks, and agricultural heart of Andhra Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Coastal Andhra
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Amaravati – ancient Buddhist site and capital of Andhra Pradesh
  • Kotappakonda – sacred hill with a famous temple and annual festival
  • Rich legacy of Satavahana and Ikshvaku dynasties

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Krishna River – scenic riverbank with boating and picnic spots
  • Uppalapadu Bird Sanctuary – haven for migratory birds

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, lodges, and transport facilities available across the city
  • Connected via Guntur Railway Station and nearby Vijayawada Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Amaravati Stupa Historic Buddhist monument with intricate carvings and relics
Kotappakonda Temple Spiritual hilltop temple with panoramic views and cultural events
Uppalapadu Bird Sanctuary Eco-tourism spot with thousands of migratory birds
Krishna Riverfront Relaxing riverside area ideal for family outings
