Welcome to Kurnool City

Explore the gateway to Rayalaseema, rich in history, culture, and natural beauty

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Rayalaseema
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Konda Reddy Fort – iconic fort symbolizing Kurnool’s heritage
  • Belum Caves – second largest cave system in India with Buddhist relics
  • Rich history as a former capital of Andhra State

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Orvakal Rock Garden – scenic park with unique rock formations
  • Rollapadu Wildlife Sanctuary – habitat for the endangered Great Indian Bustard

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and transport facilities available
  • Connected via Kurnool Railway Station and nearby airports in Hyderabad and Bengaluru

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Konda Reddy Fort Historic fort offering views and cultural significance
Belum Caves Natural underground caves with stunning formations and ancient history
Orvakal Rock Garden Park with surreal rock formations and picnic spots
Rollapadu Sanctuary Wildlife reserve known for birdwatching and conservation
