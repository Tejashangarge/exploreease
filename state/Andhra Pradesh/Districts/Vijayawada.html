Welcome to Vijayawada City

Experience the vibrant culture, history, and scenic beauty of Andhra Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: On the banks of Krishna River, Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Coastal Andhra
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Kanaka Durga Temple – a revered shrine atop Indrakeeladri Hills
  • Undavalli Caves – ancient rock-cut caves with Buddhist influences
  • Rich history tied to the Satavahana dynasty and colonial trade

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Prakasam Barrage – scenic bridge across Krishna River with boating
  • Bhavani Island – lush river island ideal for picnics and water sports

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Well-developed hotels, resorts, and transport facilities
  • Connected via Vijayawada International Airport, railway junction, and highways

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kanaka Durga Temple Spiritual temple offering panoramic views of the city and river
Undavalli Caves Historic caves with intricate carvings and Buddhist relics
Prakasam Barrage Architectural marvel and popular sunset spot
