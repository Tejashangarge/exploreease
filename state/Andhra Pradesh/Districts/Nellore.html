Welcome to Nellore City

Explore the coastal charm, cultural heritage, and natural beauty of Andhra Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Andhra Pradesh, India
  • Region: Coastal Andhra
  • Language: Telugu, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ranganatha Temple – ancient temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu
  • Rich history dating back to the Mauryan and Chola periods
  • Known for its literary contributions and spiritual traditions

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Penchalakona – scenic hill temple surrounded by forest
  • Nelapattu Bird Sanctuary – haven for migratory birds like pelicans
  • Mypadu Beach – tranquil beach ideal for relaxation

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and transport facilities available
  • Connected via Nellore Railway Station and nearby airports in Tirupati and Chennai

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ranganatha Temple Historic temple with Dravidian architecture and spiritual ambiance
