Welcome to Gandhinagar City

Explore the serene capital of Gujarat with its green spaces and cultural landmarks

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North-central Gujarat, India
  • Region: Along the banks of the Sabarmati River
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Capital city named after Mahatma Gandhi
  • Planned city with wide roads and abundant greenery
  • Hub for government institutions and cultural centers

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Indroda Nature Park – known as India’s Jurassic Park
  • Children’s Park – popular recreational spot with boating and rides

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Connected via Ahmedabad Airport and Gandhinagar Railway Station
  • Hotels, gardens, museums, and convention centers

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Akshardham Temple Magnificent temple dedicated to Swaminarayan with exhibitions and gardens
Indroda Nature Park Dinosaur fossil park with zoo, botanical garden, and science center
Mahatma Mandir Convention center and memorial dedicated to Mahatma Gandhi
Children’s Park Family-friendly park with boating, rides, and green spaces
