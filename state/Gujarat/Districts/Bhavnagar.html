Welcome to Bhavnagar City

Discover the coastal charm and royal heritage of Gujarat

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Gujarat, India
  • Region: Kathiawar Peninsula
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded by Bhavsinhji Gohil in 1723
  • Known for its princely legacy and palaces
  • Center for Jain culture and temples

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Gaurishankar Lake – scenic spot for relaxation
  • Victoria Park – lush forest reserve with wildlife

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, eateries, and local markets
  • Connected via Bhavnagar Airport and railway station

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Takhteshwar Temple Hilltop temple offering panoramic views
Gaurishankar Lake Peaceful lake surrounded by gardens
Victoria Park Nature reserve with walking trails and wildlife
Nilambag Palace Royal residence turned heritage hotel
