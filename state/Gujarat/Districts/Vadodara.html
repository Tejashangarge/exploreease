Welcome to Vadodara City

Experience the royal elegance and cultural richness of Gujarat

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Gujarat, India
  • Region: Banks of the Vishwamitri River
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former capital of the Gaekwad princely state
  • Known for its palaces, museums, and art institutions
  • Blend of Maratha heritage and modern urban culture

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Sayaji Garden – expansive park with zoo and planetarium
  • Sardar Patel Planetarium – educational and scenic spot

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Vadodara Airport and major railway station connectivity
  • Hotels, shopping malls, and cultural centers

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Laxmi Vilas Palace Grand royal residence with Indo-Saracenic architecture
Baroda Museum & Picture Gallery Historic museum with art, sculpture, and natural history
Sayaji Garden Large public garden with zoo, toy train, and planetarium
Maharaja Fateh Singh Museum Art museum housed in a royal palace
