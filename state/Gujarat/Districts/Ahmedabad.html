Welcome to Ahmedabad City

Discover the heritage, innovation, and vibrant life of Gujarat’s largest city

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Gujarat, India
  • Region: Western India
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Declared India’s first UNESCO World Heritage City
  • Founded by Sultan Ahmed Shah in 1411
  • Blend of Indo-Islamic, colonial, and modern architecture

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Kankaria Lake – recreational lakefront with zoo and amusement park
  • Sabarmati Riverfront – scenic promenade with gardens and events

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • International airport, metro rail, and extensive road network
  • Wide range of hotels, malls, eateries, and cultural venues

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Sabarmati Ashram Historic residence of Mahatma Gandhi and center of India’s freedom movement
Adalaj Stepwell Intricately carved 15th-century stepwell with Indo-Islamic architecture
Jama Masjid Grand mosque built in 1424 with stunning yellow sandstone architecture
Science City Interactive science museum and IMAX theater for all ages
