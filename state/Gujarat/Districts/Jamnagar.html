Welcome to Jamnagar City

Explore the coastal beauty and royal heritage of Gujarat

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Western Gujarat, India
  • Region: Kathiawar Peninsula
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Founded by Jam Rawal in the 16th century
  • Known for its princely state legacy and palaces
  • Famous for brass industries and Bandhani textiles

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Marine National Park – India's first marine sanctuary
  • Lakhota Lake – scenic lake with a historic fort in the center

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Well-connected by Jamnagar Airport and railway station
  • Hotels, local markets, and traditional eateries available

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Lakhota Fort Historic fort and museum located in the middle of Lakhota Lake
Marine National Park India’s first marine sanctuary with coral reefs and marine life
Bala Hanuman Temple Famous for continuous chanting of “Ram Dhun” since 1964
Khijadiya Bird Sanctuary Wetland sanctuary with diverse bird species
