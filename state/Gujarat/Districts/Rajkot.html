Welcome to Rajkot City

Experience the vibrant culture, history, and modern charm of Gujarat

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Saurashtra region, Gujarat, India
  • Region: Kathiawar Peninsula
  • Language: Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi and home to his early education
  • Rich heritage blending Hindu, Jain, and Islamic traditions
  • Known for traditional crafts, music, and festivals

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Aji Dam – peaceful reservoir with gardens and wildlife
  • Race Course – popular green space for recreation and events

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Wide range of hotels, restaurants, and shopping centers
  • Connected via Rajkot Airport and Rajkot Junction Railway Station

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Rotary Dolls Museum Unique museum showcasing dolls from around the world
Watson Museum Historic museum with artifacts from colonial and regional history
Kaba Gandhi No Delo Gandhi’s childhood home, now a museum
Aji Dam Scenic dam with gardens and a mini zoo
