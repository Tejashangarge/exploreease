Welcome to Kutch District

Uncover the vast landscapes, vibrant crafts, and desert festivals of Gujarat

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northwestern Gujarat, India
  • Region: Great Rann of Kutch and surrounding desert
  • Language: Kutchi, Gujarati, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Known for the Rann Utsav – a desert festival of music, dance, and crafts
  • Rich in tribal traditions, embroidery, and folk art
  • Historic forts and palaces from the Jadeja dynasty

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • White Desert – surreal salt marsh landscape
  • Banni Grasslands – biodiversity hotspot and birding paradise

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Bhuj Airport and railway station provide access
  • Eco-resorts, desert camps, and handicraft villages

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
White Rann of Kutch Expansive salt desert famous for moonlit views and Rann Utsav
Aina Mahal 18th-century palace with mirror work and Indo-European design
Bhujodi Village Artisan village known for weaving and embroidery
