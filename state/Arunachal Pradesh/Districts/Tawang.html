Welcome to Tawang

Explore the spiritual heights and Himalayan beauty of Arunachal Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Western Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Monpa, Hindi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the famous Tawang Monastery, one of the largest in India
  • Rich Tibetan Buddhist heritage and Monpa tribal culture
  • Strategic and spiritual importance near Indo-China border

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Sela Pass – high-altitude pass with stunning snow views
  • Madhuri Lake – glacial lake surrounded by mountains

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Guesthouses, homestays, and eco-lodges available
  • Connected via road from Tezpur and Guwahati

🏞️ Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Tawang Monastery Spiritual center with ancient scriptures and panoramic views
Sela Pass Snow-covered pass offering breathtaking Himalayan vistas
Madhuri Lake Crystal-clear lake named after a Bollywood film shoot
Gorsam Chorten Stupa resembling Nepal’s Boudhanath, located in Zemithang
