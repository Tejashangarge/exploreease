Welcome to Namdapha

Explore the wild heart of Arunachal Pradesh in India's easternmost rainforest

📍 Overview

  • Location: Changlang District, Southeastern Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Eastern Himalayas and Indo-Myanmar border
  • Language: Tangsa, Hindi, English

🦜 Ecological & Cultural Significance

  • Namdapha National Park is India’s largest protected area in the Eastern Himalayas
  • Home to four big cat species: tiger, leopard, snow leopard, and clouded leopard
  • Rich tribal culture including Tangsa and Lisu communities

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Dense tropical rainforest with rare orchids and medicinal plants
  • Noa-Dihing River – lifeline of the park and scenic picnic spot
  • Birdwatching paradise with hornbills, parrotbills, and laughingthrushes

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Forest rest houses and eco-camps available near Deban and Miao
  • Connected via road from Dibrugarh and Tinsukia; nearest airport is Mohanbari

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Namdapha National Park Vast rainforest with rare wildlife and trekking trails
Deban Forest Camp Base camp for exploring the park, located on Noa-Dihing River
Hornbill Habitat Zones Prime birdwatching areas for rare and endemic species
Miao Museum Displays tribal artifacts and biodiversity exhibits
