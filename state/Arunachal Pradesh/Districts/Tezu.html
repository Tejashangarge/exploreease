Welcome to Tezu

Discover the gateway to Lohit Valley and the cultural charm of Arunachal Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Lohit District, Eastern Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Foothills of Mishmi Hills
  • Language: Mishmi, Hindi, English

🎎 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the Mishmi tribe with rich traditions and festivals
  • Known for the Parshuram Kund pilgrimage site
  • Celebrates Tamladu Festival with vibrant rituals and dances

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Lohit River – scenic river ideal for rafting and riverside walks
  • Tezu Botanical Garden – lush garden with native flora
  • Glow Lake – high-altitude lake surrounded by forested hills

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and government lodges available
  • Connected via Tezu Airport and road from Tinsukia and Dibrugarh

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Parshuram Kund Holy site on the Lohit River, visited during Makar Sankranti
Glow Lake Remote alpine lake offering trekking and nature photography
Tezu Museum and Craft Centre Showcases Mishmi textiles, crafts, and cultural artifacts
Lohit View Point Panoramic lookout over the river valley and surrounding hills
