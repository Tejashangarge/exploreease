Welcome to Itanagar

Discover the cultural capital and scenic gateway of Arunachal Pradesh

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Himalayan Foothills
  • Language: Nyishi, Hindi, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Capital city blending tribal heritage with modern development
  • Home to the Ita Fort, built from bricks in the 14th–15th century
  • Center for festivals like Nyokum and Dree

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ganga Lake (Gyakar Sinyi) – serene lake surrounded by forest
  • Itanagar Wildlife Sanctuary – lush habitat for diverse flora and fauna

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, guesthouses, and government lodges available
  • Connected via road from Guwahati and Lilabari Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ita Fort Historic fort built with bricks, giving the city its name
Ganga Lake Peaceful lake ideal for picnics and nature walks
Jawaharlal Nehru State Museum Showcases tribal artifacts, textiles, and archaeological finds
Itanagar Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area with rich biodiversity and trekking trails
