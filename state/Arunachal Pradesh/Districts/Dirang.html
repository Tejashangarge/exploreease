Welcome to Dirang

Experience the tranquil valleys and cultural richness of Arunachal Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: West Kameng District, Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Monpa, Hindi, English

🧘 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the Monpa tribe and Buddhist monasteries
  • Known for traditional stone houses and ancient Dirang Dzong
  • Peaceful gateway to Tawang and high-altitude passes

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Dirang Valley – scenic landscape with rivers, fields, and hills
  • Dirang Hot Springs – natural geothermal pools with healing properties
  • Sangti Valley – lush valley known for black-necked cranes and camping

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Homestays, guesthouses, and eco-lodges available
  • Connected via road from Tezpur and Bomdila

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Dirang Dzong Historic fort-like village with stone houses and Buddhist heritage
Dirang Hot Springs Natural thermal pools popular for relaxation and wellness
Sangti Valley Scenic valley with birdwatching, camping, and river views
Thupsung Dhargye Ling Monastery Modern monastery offering spiritual teachings and panoramic views
