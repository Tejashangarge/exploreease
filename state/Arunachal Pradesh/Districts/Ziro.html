Welcome to Ziro

Explore the tranquil rice fields and tribal heritage of Arunachal Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: Lower Subansiri District, Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Ziro Valley, Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Apatani, Hindi, English

🧬 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to the Apatani tribe known for sustainable farming and facial tattoos
  • Hosts the famous Ziro Music Festival blending indie music and nature
  • UNESCO tentative site for cultural landscape recognition

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Ziro Valley – lush rice fields, pine forests, and gentle hills
  • Talley Valley Wildlife Sanctuary – biodiversity hotspot with trekking trails

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Homestays, eco-lodges, and campsites available
  • Connected via road from Itanagar and nearest railhead at Naharlagun

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ziro Valley Scenic valley with rice paddies and Apatani villages
Talley Valley Sanctuary Protected forest area with rich flora and fauna
Ziro Music Festival Grounds Annual venue for indie music amidst nature
Hong Village One of the largest Apatani settlements showcasing traditional lifestyle
