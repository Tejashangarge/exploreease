Welcome to Pasighat

Experience the gateway to Arunachal Pradesh and the banks of the Siang River

📍 Overview

  • Location: East Siang District, Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Foothills of the Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Adi, Hindi, English

🏯 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Oldest town in Arunachal Pradesh, founded in 1911
  • Home to the Adi tribe and vibrant Solung Festival
  • Blend of tribal traditions and colonial heritage

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Siang River – ideal for rafting, fishing, and riverside picnics
  • Daying Ering Wildlife Sanctuary – wetland habitat for migratory birds
  • Arunachal Pradesh’s lush tea gardens and forested hills

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels, eco-resorts, and homestays available
  • Connected via Pasighat Airport and road from Dibrugarh and Itanagar

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Siang River Majestic river offering adventure sports and scenic views
Daying Ering Wildlife Sanctuary Wetland sanctuary with migratory birds and riverine islands
Pasighat View Point Panoramic lookout over the valley and river confluence
Solung Festival Grounds Venue for traditional Adi celebrations and cultural performances
