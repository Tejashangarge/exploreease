Welcome to Bomdila

Discover the tranquil monasteries and mountain vistas of Arunachal Pradesh

📍 Overview

  • Location: West Kameng District, Arunachal Pradesh, India
  • Region: Eastern Himalayas
  • Language: Monpa, Hindi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Center of Tibetan Buddhist culture and Monpa traditions
  • Home to Bomdila Monastery and vibrant Losar Festival
  • Gateway to Tawang and high-altitude Himalayan passes

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Bomdila View Point – panoramic views of snow-capped ranges
  • Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary – biodiversity hotspot for birdwatchers
  • Apple orchards and alpine forests surrounding the town

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Monastery guesthouses, homestays, and budget hotels available
  • Connected via road from Tezpur and Guwahati

📌 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Bomdila Monastery Peaceful monastery with prayer halls and cultural significance
Bomdila View Point Scenic lookout offering views of the Himalayas and valleys
Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary Protected forest area known for rare bird species and biodiversity
Craft Center & Ethnographic Museum Showcases Monpa handicrafts, textiles, and cultural artifacts
