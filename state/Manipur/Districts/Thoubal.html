Welcome to Thoubal

Explore the lakes, rivers and rich heritage of Manipur

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Thoubal River valley
  • Language: Meiteilon (Manipuri), English, Hindi

🏛️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Khongjom War Memorial – Site of Anglo-Manipur War 1891
  • People's Museum showcasing Manipuri culture and artifacts
  • Panthoibi Temple and Shri Govindjee Temple

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Waithou Lake – Pristine lake surrounded by hills
  • Loukoi Pat Lake – Scenic spot for boating and picnics
  • Thoubal River with lush paddy fields

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Local homestays and lakeside accommodations
  • Connected via NH-102, 50 km from Imphal Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Khongjom War Memorial Monument to Manipuri warriors atop scenic hill
Waithou Lake Beautiful lake perfect for boating and photography
People's Museum Cultural artifacts and Manipuri heritage exhibits
Loukoi Pat Lake Tranquil lake surrounded by greenery
