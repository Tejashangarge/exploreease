Discover the cultural heartland and natural beauty of Manipur

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Imphal Valley
  • Language: Meiteilon (Manipuri), English, Hindi

💃 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Iboyi Shaolin Temple – Center of Thang-Ta martial arts
  • Manipur State Museum – Rich tribal heritage and history
  • Royal Palace ruins – Legacy of Manipuri kings

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Keibul Lamjao National Park – Floating wetlands and Sangai deer
  • Imphal River – Scenic beauty and traditional fishing

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage hotels and homestays with Manipuri hospitality
  • Airport, rail, and excellent road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Shri Govindajee Temple Magnificent Vaishnavite temple with golden domes
Kangla Fort Ancient seat of Manipuri kings with historical significance
