Welcome to Churachandpur

Discover tribal heritage and stunning hill landscapes of Manipur

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southwestern Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Hill district, second largest town (Lamka)
  • Language: Paite, Hmar, Meiteilon, English

🌄 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Rich Kuki-Zo tribal culture and traditions
  • Tuibuong Tribal Museum with authentic artifacts
  • Vibrant tribal festivals and handloom weaving

🏞️ Natural Attractions

  • Khuga Dam – Scenic reservoir amidst rolling hills
  • Tonglon Caves – Mysterious natural cave formations
  • Ngaloi Falls – Cascading waterfall in lush greenery

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Tribal homestays and guesthouses
  • Connected via NH-102, 2 hours from Imphal Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Khuga Dam Major dam with panoramic hill views and picnic spots
Tonglon Caves Ancient cave system perfect for adventure seekers
Ngaloi Falls Beautiful waterfall surrounded by dense forest
Tuibuong Tribal Museum Rich collection of tribal artifacts and culture
