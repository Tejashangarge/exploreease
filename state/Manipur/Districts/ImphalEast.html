Welcome to Imphal East

Experience the verdant beauty and vibrant culture of Manipur

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Imphal Valley extension
  • Language: Meiteilon (Manipuri), English, Hindi

🎭 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Yairipok – Famous for indigenous Kambong wine and crafts
  • Traditional Manipuri dance and Ras Leela performances
  • Ancient burial mounds and archaeological sites

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Loktak Lake periphery – Pristine wetlands and birdwatching
  • Lush paddy fields and rolling green hills

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Eco-resorts and village homestays
  • Excellent connectivity via NH-102 and Imphal Airport

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Sangai Deer Sanctuary Rare brow-antlered deer in floating phumdis
ISF Stadium World-famous hockey stadium and sports hub
Leimakhong Eco-Park Beautiful picnic spot with natural springs
Moreh Border Town India-Myanmar gateway with vibrant markets
