Gateway to Myanmar with rich biodiversity and tribal culture

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Southeastern Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Indo-Myanmar border district
  • Language: Various Naga dialects, Meiteilon, English

🌄 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Rich Naga and Kuki tribal heritage and traditions
  • Gateway to Myanmar via Moreh border town
  • Langol Peak Garden with panoramic valley views

🌿 Natural Attractions

  • Yangoupokpi-Lokchao Wildlife Sanctuary – Rich biodiversity
  • Tengnoupal viewpoint – Bird's eye view of Manipur Valley
  • Exotic orchids and rare medicinal plants

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Tribal homestays and border town accommodations
  • Connected via Indo-Myanmar highway, 70 km from Imphal

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Moreh Border Town India-Myanmar gateway with international trade hub
Tengnoupal Highest point on Indo-Myanmar road with valley views
Yangoupokpi-Lokchao Wildlife Sanctuary Home to clouded leopards, elephants, and rare orchids
Langol Peak Garden Scenic hilltop garden surrounded by breathtaking landscapes
