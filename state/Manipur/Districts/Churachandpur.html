Welcome to Tamenglong

Biodiversity paradise with caves, waterfalls and wildlife

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Hilly district with Barak River valley
  • Language: Zeliangrong Naga dialects, English

🌄 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Zeliangrong Naga tribal culture and festivals
  • Biodiversity hotspot with rare orchids and wildlife
  • Famous orange orchards and subtropical forests

🏞️ Natural Attractions

  • Tharon Cave – 655m long limestone cave system
  • Barak Waterfalls – Series of seven cascading falls
  • Zeilad Lake – Home to pythons, turtles and water birds

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Tribal homestays amidst scenic hill landscapes
  • 4-5 hours drive from Imphal via NH-202

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Tharon Cave Ancient 655m limestone cave for adventure seekers
Barak Waterfalls Spectacular series of seven waterfalls
Zeilad Lake Wildlife-rich lake with rare python population
Buning Meadow Pristine meadow paradise for nature lovers
