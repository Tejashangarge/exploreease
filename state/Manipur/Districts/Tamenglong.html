Welcome to Senapati

Land of butterflies in northern Manipur hills

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Hill district bordering Nagaland
  • Language: Mao Naga, Tangkhul, Meiteilon, English

🌄 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Mao and Tangkhul Naga tribal culture and festivals
  • Land of butterflies with rich biodiversity
  • Historical trade route connecting Manipur and Nagaland

🏞️ Natural Attractions

  • Mao-Senapati hills with butterfly sanctuaries
  • Lush forests and terraced paddy fields
  • Scenic viewpoints along Imphal-Kohima highway

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Tribal homestays and guesthouses
  • On NH-2 Imphal-Kohima highway, 60 km from Imphal

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Mao Market Vibrant tribal market with local handicrafts
Senapati Viewpoint Panoramic views of northern Manipur hills
Butterfly Valley Home to hundreds of butterfly species
Tamei Tea Estate Scenic tea gardens amidst rolling hills
