Welcome to Bishnupur

Discover the terracotta temples and royal heritage of Manipur

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Western Manipur, Northeast India
  • Region: Loktak Lake basin
  • Language: Meiteilon (Manipuri), English, Hindi

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • UNESCO-recognized terracotta temples (Ras Manchas, Shree Shree Govindaji)
  • Birthplace of Moirang Kontha crafts and Balam Khun silk weaving
  • Ancient Moirang dynasty capital with INA Museum

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Loktak Lake – Largest freshwater lake in Northeast India with floating phumdis
  • Sendra Island viewpoint and hilltop panoramas

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Heritage homestays and lakeside resorts
  • Well-connected via NH-102 and Imphal Airport (35 km)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ras Manchas Temple UNESCO terracotta pyramid temple complex
Loktak Lake Floating islands and Sangai deer sanctuary
INA Museum Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose freedom struggle memorial
Shri Shri Govindaji Temple Magnificent brick temple with intricate carvings
