Welcome to Bhubaneswar City

Explore the Temple City - Capital of Odisha

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Central Odisha, India
  • Region: Known as "Temple City of India"
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, English

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Over 700 ancient temples showcasing Kalinga architecture
  • Ancient capital of Kalinga Empire (Lingaraj Temple - 11th century)
  • Udayagiri & Khandagiri Caves - Jain heritage from 2nd century BC

🌿 Natural Attractions

  • Nandankanan Zoological Park with botanical gardens
  • Dhauli Hill with Shanti Stupa and scenic views
  • Biju Patnaik Park and eco-parks

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • International airport and major railway junction
  • Luxury hotels, heritage stays, and budget accommodations
  • Excellent road connectivity to Puri and Konark

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Lingaraj Temple 55m tall Kalinga-style Shiva temple complex
Mukteshwar Temple "Gem of Odisha" - 10th century architectural marvel
Udayagiri Caves Ancient Jain caves carved in 2nd century BC
Rajarani Temple Ornate 11th-century temple with erotic sculptures
