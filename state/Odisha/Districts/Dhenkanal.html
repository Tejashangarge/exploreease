Welcome to Dhenkanal City

Temple Trail & Waterfall Wonderland of Odisha

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Central Odisha, India
  • Region: Between Brahmani & Mahanadi Rivers
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, English

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to majestic Kapilash Temple (1246 AD)
  • Joranda Mahima Dharma spiritual center
  • Historic Dhenkanal Palace & royal heritage

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Saptasajya Hills with seven sacred peaks
  • Numerous waterfalls in lush forests
  • Baisipalli Wildlife Sanctuary nearby

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Good rail connectivity via Dhenkanal station
  • Hotels and palace heritage stays available
  • Road access to Bhubaneswar and Cuttack

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kapilash Temple Ancient Shiva temple atop 1352 steps
Saptasajya Hills Seven sacred hills with panoramic views
Joranda Mahima Temple Spiritual hub of Mahima Dharma sect
Saranga Vishnu Temple Anantasayi Vishnu on Brahmani riverbed
