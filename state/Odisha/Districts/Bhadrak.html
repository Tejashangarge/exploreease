Welcome to Bhadrak City

Discover the temple-rich coastal heritage of Odisha

🌊 Overview

  • Location: Coastal Odisha, India
  • Region: Between Dhamra River and Bay of Bengal
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, Bengali

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Aradi Akhandalamani Temple - Famous Shiva temple
  • Bhadrakali Temple - Powerful goddess shrine
  • Ancient maritime trade history with ports

🌴 Natural Attractions

  • Dhamra Port area with scenic river views
  • Nearby beaches and mangrove forests
  • Agricultural landscapes with paddy fields

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Temple guest houses and hotels
  • Rail connectivity via Bhadrak junction
  • NH-16 highway access to major cities

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Akhandalamani Temple Ancient Shiva temple on Dhamra riverbank
Bhadrakali Temple Historic goddess temple in town center
Dhamra Port Scenic fishing port with river cruise views
Bankadabasi Temple Ancient temple with unique architecture
