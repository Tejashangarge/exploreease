Welcome to Jagannath Puri

Odisha's spiritual heart & Rath Yatra capital

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: Puri District, Odisha, India
  • Region: Coastal Odisha
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, English

🏰 Historical & Spiritual Significance

  • Home to world-famous Jagannath Temple (12th century)
  • Annual Rath Yatra - Chariot Festival spectacle
  • One of Char Dham pilgrimage sites
  • Ancient Kalinga architecture marvel

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Puri Beach – golden sands & sunrise views
  • Chilika Lake – Asia's largest brackish water lagoon
  • Sakshi Gopal Temple area – serene surroundings
  • Swargadwar Beach – spiritual bathing ghat

🛣️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent rail & road connectivity (2 hrs from Bhubaneswar)
  • Wide range of hotels, resorts & OTDCs
  • Part of Golden Triangle (Puri-Konark-Bhubaneswar)

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Jagannath Temple 12th century Kalinga architecture wonder
Rath Yatra Route Historic chariot procession path
Puri Beach Golden sands with lighthouse views
Gundicha Temple Rath Yatra's return destination
