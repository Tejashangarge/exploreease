Welcome to Cuttack City

Silver City - Commercial Capital of Odisha

🏛️ Overview

  • Location: East Central Odisha, India
  • Region: On Mahanadi River delta
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, English

🛕 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Former capital of Odisha (989 CE - 1948)
  • World-famous for silver filigree work
  • Birthplace of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Mahanadi River with scenic barrages
  • Gorgeous waterfalls nearby (Dhabaleswar, Deojhar)
  • Deer Park and botanical gardens

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Excellent rail and road connectivity
  • Gateway to Bhubaneswar and Puri
  • Wide range of accommodations

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Barabati Fort 14th-century ruins on Mahanadi riverbank
Cuttack Chandi Temple Ancient Durga temple - city deity
Dhabaleswar Temple Riverside Shiva temple island
Jobra Barrage Popular Mahanadi sunset viewpoint
