Welcome to Balasore City

Discover the coastal paradise and temple treasures of Odisha

🏖️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Odisha, India
  • Region: Coastal Balasore District
  • Language: Odia, Hindi, English

🕉️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Panchalingeswar Temple - Mystical hilltop Shiva shrine
  • Ancient temples with unique waterfall lingams
  • Rich maritime heritage and fishing traditions

🌊 Natural Attractions

  • Chandipur Beach - Famous for disappearing sea phenomenon
  • Talasari Beach - Pristine sands with red crabs
  • Bhusandeswar - Giant Shiva lingam by Subarnarekha River

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Beach resorts and budget accommodations
  • Excellent rail connectivity to major cities
  • Road access to nearby wildlife sanctuaries

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Chandipur Beach Unique beach where sea recedes 5km at low tide
Panchalingeswar Temple Five Shiva lingams behind cascading waterfall
Talasari-Udaypur Beach Picturesque beach with casuarina groves
Bhusandeswar Temple 12ft tall monolithic Shiva lingam
