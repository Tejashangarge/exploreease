Welcome to Tezpur City

Explore the spiritual, historical, and scenic beauty of the City of Eternal Romance

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: North Bank of Brahmaputra, Assam, India
  • Region: Sonitpur District
  • Language: Assamese, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • City of Eternal Romance - Usha-Aniruddha love story
  • Ancient capital of King Banasura from Mahabharata era
  • Rich archaeological ruins from 4th-10th centuries

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Brahmaputra River - panoramic views and sunsets
  • Tea gardens and scenic hill viewpoints

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and resorts for all budgets
  • Rail, air, and road connectivity

Attraction Description
Agnigarh Hill Legendary hill of Usha-Aniruddha love story
Bamuni Hills Ancient 9th-10th century temple ruins
