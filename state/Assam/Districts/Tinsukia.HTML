Welcome to Tinsukia City

Explore the spiritual, historical, and scenic beauty of Assam's Oil & Tea City

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Eastern Assam, India
  • Region: Brahmaputra Valley
  • Language: Assamese, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Asia's first oil refinery town - Digboi nearby
  • Rich Ahom kingdom heritage and monuments
  • Vibrant tea plantation culture

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Brahmaputra River - scenic river views
  • Lush tea estates and wildlife sanctuaries

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and resorts for all budgets
  • Rail, air, and road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Dibru-Saikhowa National Park River island park with feral horses
Na-Pukhuri (Nine Ponds) Ahom-era cluster of historical ponds
Tilinga Mandir (Bell Temple) Temple covered with thousands of bells
Maguri Motapung Beel Birdwatcher's wetland paradise
