  • Tea City of India – heart of Assam's tea plantations

  • Brahmaputra River – scenic riverfront views
  • Expansive tea gardens and lush greenery

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and resorts for all budgets
  • Rail, air, and road connectivity
