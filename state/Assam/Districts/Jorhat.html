Welcome to Jorhat City

Explore the spiritual, historical, and scenic beauty of Assam's Cultural Capital

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Upper Assam, India
  • Region: Brahmaputra Valley
  • Language: Assamese, Hindi, English

🕌 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Cultural Capital of Assam and Tea Capital of India
  • Last capital of the mighty Ahom Kingdom
  • Gateway to world's largest river island - Majuli

🌄 Natural Attractions

  • Expansive tea gardens and lush plantations
  • Brahmaputra River - scenic ferry rides to Majuli

🏨 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Hotels and resorts for all budgets
  • Rail, air, and road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Tocklai Tea Research Institute World's oldest and largest tea research center
Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary Home to India's only ape species - Hoolock Gibbon
Dhekiakhowa Bornamghar Oldest Sattra (Vaishnavite monastery) in Assam
Raja Maidam Ahom royal burial mound with historical significance
