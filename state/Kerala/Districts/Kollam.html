Welcome to Kollam

Prince of God’s Own Country - Backwaters & Cashew Coast

🌅 Overview

  • Location: Southern Kerala, India
  • Region: Gateway to Kerala's backwaters
  • Language: Malayalam, English, Tamil

⛩️ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Ancient port city with Portuguese & Dutch heritage
  • World's largest copra & cashew processing hub
  • Oachira Temple - unique idol-less pilgrimage site

🚤 Natural Attractions

  • Ashtamudi Lake - Kerala’s deepest backwater lake
  • Thangassery Beach with 144ft iconic lighthouse
  • Palaruvi Waterfalls amidst medicinal forests

🛶 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Houseboat cruises & luxury resorts
  • Jatayu Earth Centre adventure park
  • Excellent rail & road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Ashtamudi Lake Palm-shaped backwaters with houseboat rides
Thangassery Lighthouse 144ft tall historic beacon with sea views
Palaruvi Falls 300ft "Stream of Milk" waterfall
Jatayu Earth Centre World's largest bird sculpture & adventure park
