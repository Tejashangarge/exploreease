Welcome to Idukki

Kerala's Spice Garden - Land of High Ranges

🌄 Overview

  • Location: Eastern Kerala highlands, Western Ghats
  • Region: Kerala's largest district by area
  • Language: Malayalam, Tamil, English

🍵 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Kerala's Spice Garden - tea, coffee, cardamom hub
  • Home to Munnar & Thekkady hill stations
  • Asia's largest arch dam engineering marvel

🦏 Natural Attractions

  • Periyar Tiger Reserve & Idukki Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Tea estates, waterfalls & mist-covered hills
  • Neelakurinji flowers bloom once every 12 years

🚡 Tourism Infrastructure

  • Treehouses, spice plantation resorts & campsites
  • Trekking, bamboo rafting & wildlife safaris
  • Access via Kochi airport & Thekkady road

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Idukki Arch Dam Asia's largest arch dam between two mountains
Munnar Tea Gardens World-famous rolling hills of emerald tea
Periyar Tiger Reserve Tiger & elephant safaris on Periyar Lake
Thommankuthu Falls 7-step cascading waterfalls with caves
