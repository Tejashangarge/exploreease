Welcome to Wayanad

Queen of Hills - Nature's Paradise

🏞️ Overview

  • Location: Northern Kerala hills, Western Ghats
  • Region: Part of Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve
  • Language: Malayalam, English, Kannada

🦒 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Home to ancient Edakkal Caves (Stone Age carvings)
  • Pazhassi Raja - Lion of Kerala fought British here
  • Tribal heritage of Paniya, Kattunaikka communities

🌲 Natural Attractions

  • Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary - elephants, tigers, leopards
  • Chembra Peak - heart-shaped lake trek
  • Banasura Sagar Dam - largest earth dam in India

🛤️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Treehouses, jungle resorts & homestays
  • Trekking, zip-lining, safaris available
  • Well-connected via Calicut airport & rail

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Chembra Peak Highest peak with heart-shaped lake
Edakkal Caves 7000-year-old prehistoric carvings
Banasura Sagar Dam Asia's 2nd largest earth dam
Soochipara Falls Sentinel Rock Falls - trekking paradise
