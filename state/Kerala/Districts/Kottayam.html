Welcome to Kottayam

Land of Backwaters, Hills & Rubber Plantations

🌿 Overview

  • Location: Central Kerala, India
  • Region: Heart of Kerala's plantation district
  • Language: Malayalam, English, Hindi

⛪ Cultural & Historical Significance

  • City of Letters - Home to Malayala Manorama & Deepika
  • First district in India to achieve 100% literacy
  • Rich Christian heritage with ancient churches

🚤 Natural Attractions

  • Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary on Vembanad Lake
  • Vagamon & Ilaveezhapoonchira hill stations
  • Marmala & Aruvikkuzhi waterfalls

🏞️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury houseboats & eco-resorts
  • Adventure trekking & paragliding
  • Well-connected by rail & road

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary 14-acre paradise for migratory birds
Ilaveezhapoonchira Three hillocks with monsoon lake views
Vagamon Hill Station Meadows, pine forests & paragliding
Marmala Waterfall Picturesque falls amidst rubber estates
