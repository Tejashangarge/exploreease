Welcome to Kochi

Discover the Queen of the Arabian Sea - Kerala

🏝️ Overview

  • Location: Ernakulam District, Kerala, India
  • Region: Central Kerala Coast
  • Language: Malayalam, English, Hindi

🌊 Cultural & Historical Significance

  • Fort Kochi - Portuguese, Dutch & British colonial heritage
  • China Fishing Nets - iconic 600-year-old fishing method
  • Jewish Synagogue - one of the oldest active synagogues

🌴 Natural Attractions

  • Arabian Sea coastline with golden beaches
  • Backwaters and houseboat experiences
  • Lush coconut groves and tropical landscapes

🛥️ Tourism Infrastructure

  • Luxury resorts, heritage homestays & budget hotels
  • International airport, major port & excellent road connectivity

📍 Must-Visit Attractions

Attraction Description
Fort Kochi Colonial architecture & Chinese fishing nets
Mattancherry Palace Dutch Palace with stunning murals
St. Francis Church Oldest European church in India
Kerala Kathakali Centre Traditional dance-drama performances
