Tiruvanthpuram – Land of Temples

Tiruvanthpuram, capital of Kerala, blends ancient temple heritage, serene beaches, lush greenery and space research hub status, offering cultural depth with modern scientific achievements.

Temples • Beaches • Ayurveda • Space City

Major attractions & experiences

Padmanabhaswamy Temple headlines with its towering gopuram and immense wealth, alongside Kovalam Beach, Napier Museum, Shanghumugham Beach and Science & Technology Museum complex.

Cultural gems include Kerala Secretariat, Attukal Temple, Chowara Beach, plus nearby Poovar estuary and Varkala cliff beach destination.

Padmanabhaswamy Temple
18th-century Vishnu temple famed for 18-ft reclining idol and legendary hidden treasures.
Kovalam Lighthouse Beach
World-famous crescent beach with lighthouse, resorts, ayurveda centers and seafood.
Napier Museum
Indo-Saracenic architecture housing natural history, art and bronze collections.
Shanghumugham Beach
Popular city beach with mermaid statue, cafes, evening promenade and sea views.

City overview & history

Tiruvanthpuram serves as Kerala's capital and administrative center, renowned for its rich cultural heritage, literacy and India's premier space research facility Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre.

Name meaning "City of Lord Anantha," founded around the sacred Padmanabhaswamy Temple, evolved under Travancore Kingdom with European trade influences.

Known as Kerala's cultural capital with highest literacy rate, major IT parks and gateway to Kerala's famous backwaters and beaches.

Quick facts

  • Region: Southern coastal Kerala.
  • Key identity: Temple city, space research and cultural capital.
  • Population: Over 1.7 million in city area.
  • Languages: Malayalam, English, Tamil.

Climate & best time to visit

Tropical monsoon climate with high humidity, abundant rainfall and pleasant temperatures moderated by Arabian Sea breezes throughout the year.

Ideal visiting season spans October to March with comfortable weather perfect for temple visits, beach relaxation and cultural festivals.

Heavy southwest monsoon June-September with 2000mm+ rainfall; summer April-May hot and humid reaching 34°C.

Best season: Oct – Mar.
Summer highs: 32–35°C.
Monsoon: Jun – Sep.

Culture, food & lifestyle

Heart of Kerala culture with Kathakali dance, Kalaripayattu martial arts, Onam harvest festival, classical music and temple rituals dominating daily life.

Authentic Kerala cuisine featuring appam-stew, fish curry-rice, banana leaf meals, payasam and toddy served in thatched beach shacks.

Famous ayurveda wellness tradition, handloom textiles, Kathakali theaters and highest literacy nurturing progressive society.

Travel tips for visitors

Plan 3 days for city temples/museums, 2 days Kovalam relaxation; use KSRTC buses, autos or cabs; extend for Varkala, Alleppey backwaters or Kanyakumari.

  • Temple visits require strict dress code (dhoti for men, saree/churidar for women).
  • Year-round beach weather; monsoon offers dramatic ocean views but strong currents.
  • Try authentic Kerala sadhya meals; carry mosquito repellent for evenings.
